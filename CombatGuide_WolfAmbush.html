Combat Reference — Wolf Ambush

Blighted wolf pack — campsite reveal — Level 2 party of 5 — Moderate, front-loaded by surprise
Encounter behaviour

Play this as a "setting up camp" scene first — ask where tents go, who's on watch, who's still got hands full of firewood or gear — before revealing the pack. The reveal should land as a genuine surprise round if the party hasn't set a watch or taken other precautions.

These are starving, sick animals, not tactical combatants. The four smaller wolves are already at half their true HP and will drop in one or two hits each. The Alpha is the reason they're still alive at all — tougher, and the one most likely to keep fighting if the pack starts to break.

Wrongness, not exposition. Describe patchy fur, sores, and wounds that look unnaturally old rather than fresh. Nothing overt or explained — just visibly wrong. If Arjay examines a body or is bitten, he feels an echo of something familiar without understanding why.

Terrain — sparse, unhealthy forest clearing
Thin canopyWeak light, patchy leaf cover overhead — doesn't meaningfully obstruct sightlines.
Dead undergrowth and rootsUneven ground and fallen, rotting logs offer minor difficult terrain and low cover around the campsite.
Camp set-upWhatever the party's placed — tents, packs, a fire pit — becomes incidental terrain and can block or grant partial cover depending on where people were standing when the pack arrives.

Atk+4
Spd40
Each turn
Bite — +4 / 2d6+2 piercing DC 12 Str save or knocked prone
Abilities
Pack Tactics — Advantage on an attack roll if an ally is within 5 ft. of the same target and not incapacitated.
Desperate Frenzy — Advantage on its first attack roll each combat.
Tactics
Hangs back a beat while the smaller wolves close in first, then commits. Does not break and flee even if the rest of the pack scatters — this one keeps fighting.
