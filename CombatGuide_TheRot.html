Combat Reference — The Rot

Four tiers of accumulated decay — they spread rather than kill — October 2026 rules
The Rotten condition

Stacks. A creature gains a stack each time it fails a save against the Rot. Each stack lowers one ability score by 2, always in this order: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. Modifiers, saves, attacks, AC and HP follow the score.

Speed. From the first stack, speed is reduced by 10 ft. This does not increase with more stacks.

When to save. A Constitution save on contact with Rot and at the start of each of the creature's turns while still in contact.

Cap. Six stacks. Any further infestation at six drops the creature to 0 HP and normal dying rules apply. Recovering without magical healing leaves it at six.

Removal. Only magical healing (spell, prayer, potion or the ring) removes every stack at once. The Rot is forced out as a single dead Rot Dreg sized to the stacks removed. Rest removes nothing. There is no save to shake it off.

Immunity. Part-deified beings (Arjay, Dohyuk, Huw) never gain stacks, and healing expels nothing from them.

Rot DC by source (add 1 per stack already held; highest base if several)
CreaturesRot Dreg 10, Rot Skulker 12, Rot Beast 14, Rot Monstrosity 17.
Not a creatureAn infested creature's blood, a contaminated object or residue: 10. Rot growths and seals have HP (20 for the floor 4 door seal), take full physical damage and double fire damage, and are cleared instantly by salt. Striking one barehanded, including unarmed strikes, is a DC 10 Con save. Weapons do not count.
SubmergedBelow the waist 12, waist to head 14, fully under 17.
Damage interactions, all tiers
Radiant, fireVulnerable.
NecroticHeals the Rot by the amount dealt.
AcidNormal damage, and triggers Effluent: Rot sprays out (5 ft).
LightningNormal damage, and triggers Effluent at 10 ft with a higher DC.
ThunderNormal damage, and triggers Thunder Fission: a new Rot creature splits off. Fragment bands: up to 6 HP Dreg, up to 18 Skulker, up to 45 Beast.
ColdNormal damage plus Slowed. Reduced to 0 HP by cold, the Rot freezes instead of dying.
BludgeoningResistant, plus Slowed. Slashing, piercing: resistant, and cutting it open means the next fire, radiant or acid damage it takes counts as vulnerable.
Force, poisonNormal.
PsychicImmune (Dreg, Skulker), resistant (Beast, Monstrosity). No psychic effects ever apply.
SaltNot a damage type, but the Rot cannot withstand it. Destroys frozen Rot, cleans contaminated objects and breaks Rot seals.
Slowed2024 D&D has no Slowed condition. Rule it as speed halved until the end of the creature's next turn.
Shared traits, all tiers
Living RotNo mind. Can't be targeted by effects that need cognition, language or psychology. Fear, commands and other cognitive riders never apply.
SeepMoves through gaps as small as one inch without squeezing.
Cold SuspensionReduced to 0 HP by cold, it freezes instead of being destroyed, and recovers to full HP after 1 hour unless fire, radiant damage or salt destroys it first. Cold damage also Slows it until the end of its next turn.
Thunder FissionThunder damage expels a fragment with HP equal to half the thunder damage, which joins initiative immediately as the tier matching its HP (up to 6 Dreg, up to 18 Skulker, up to 45 Beast). No fragment if the thunder damage drops it to 0 HP.
Rot Dreg
Tiny ooze, mouse-sized, no chosen actions
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing. Immune: necrotic (heals it), psychic. Immune to blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, prone, restrained, unconscious, incapacitated.
Tactics
Hazard rather than fight. It drifts toward the nearest living thing and harms by contact. Place it where a careless step matters.
Rot Skulker
Small ooze, large rat to small cat, lunges at targets
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing. Immune: necrotic (heals it), psychic. Immune to blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, prone, restrained, unconscious, incapacitated.
Tactics
Moves to the nearest living creature and lunges. No strategy, only intent. Does not retreat. Strikes from cover where Seep allows an ambush.
Rot Beast
Medium ooze, pig-sized, attacks to spread
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing, psychic. Immune: necrotic (heals it). Immune to blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, prone, restrained, unconscious.
Tactics
Surges at the nearest creature and attacks to spread rather than kill. Does not retreat. Not used in the Everbloom catacombs.
Rot Monstrosity
Large ooze, 10 ft across, survival instinct, legendary actions
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing, psychic. Immune: necrotic (heals it). Immune to blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, prone, restrained, unconscious.
