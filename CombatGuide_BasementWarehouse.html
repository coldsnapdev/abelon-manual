Combat Reference — The Basement Warehouse

Tomas and Pol vs the party — underground, cramped, witnesses present — Level 2 party
Encounter behaviour

This fight only happens if the party tips Tomas from frustrated to committed. It is not inevitable. The trigger is a dismissive tone, an implied threat, reaching for a weapon too early, or being spoken over. Watch for the moment and let it land naturally.

Once it starts, Tomas fights to kill. He does not yield. Pol breaks if Tomas goes down — he uses the Dodge action rather than continuing to attack. He is not Tomas. He just wanted to belong to something.

Dask moves Marta toward the locked room when things escalate. He does not fight. The other family members stay at the edges. Sem's bedroll is still visible. Do not narrate it.

Tomas
Commoner — fights to kill, does not yield
AC11
HP16
Atk+3
Spd30
Above half HP — advantage on all attack rolls. He is fully committed and he knows this space.
Each turn
Improvised weapon or unarmed — +3 / 1d4+2 bludgeoning
Abilities
Home advantage — Knows every corner of the basement. Uses alcoves and low beams to force the party to come to him.
Relentless — Does not disengage, retreat, or yield under any circumstances. Must be incapacitated to stop.
Tactics
Goes for whoever spoke to him last. Stays in the centre of the room to deny movement. Uses the tight space — the party cannot spread out. Never asks for quarter and does not accept it.
Pol

AC10
HP9
Atk+2

Abilities
Breaks if Tomas falls — Immediately switches to Dodge action when Tomas is incapacitated. Does not flee. Just stops.
Tactics
Follows Tomas entirely. Attacks whoever Tomas is not attacking. Commits less than he appears to. Separate him from Tomas and he becomes significantly less dangerous.
