Combat Reference — Assassination Paths

The night before the signing. Each target fights alone. Level 4 party of five.
Running the fights

Every target fights alone. Five against one isn't a fair fight, and that's the point: if the party wins, it's because they outnumbered their target, not because they were better. Five on one is a hard fight, not a deadly one; two or three on one is genuinely dangerous, and if the party splits, let them live with that choice.

Seva runs, Oskar doesn't, Alric buys time. The fights end differently: a chase, a last stand or a smoke-filled stalemate. A party that scouts, cuts off exits or catches a target off guard should feel the benefit. None of them begs.

Seva Sable / The Amber Cup, private area

If the party sided with Alric

The private area off the busy main room where Seva met the party. Mira stops by every so often and does not fight; a watchful party knows when she is there. The crowd covers a short fight, not a long one. Below 41 HP Seva leaves through the nearest exit, so the kill depends on who is covering the ways out.

Seva Sable
Tactics
Opens with Whispered Doubt on the strongest attacker, aimed at whoever they trust most; Danzel first if he is there. Attacks, then Hides or Disengages every turn. Below 41 HP she uses The Back Stair and runs.

Oskar Sternov / Honeywell, after hours

If the party sided with Seva

The forge floor below the Honeywell office, the forge banked and still hot. The party arrives as Oskar is briefing Bram; a stealthy party can overhear it (Oskar passive Perception 11, Bram 13). Bram then leaves for Alric and Oskar is alone, with no apprentices. He keeps the forge at his back and the party between him and the street door. Thrown into the open forge: an extra 2d6 fire. Banked Coals works only within 10 feet of the forge.

Oskar Sternov
Tactics
Targets whoever hurts him most. Grapples small, quick characters and hurls them into their friends or the coals. Doesn't flee: he fights until he drops, and Not Today makes the first apparent kill a false one.

Alric Venn / The Shallow Tuber, private area

Alric and Bram

Nobody hires the party to go after Alric, but it isn't impossible. He stays in the private area at the busy Shallow Tuber. Bram goes for him tonight, lands one punch and runs, which is why Alric has a bruise at the signing. A party also after Alric notices Bram, either working up to it in the common room or mid-attempt, and can meet him there. Otherwise it happens off camera.

Alric Venn
Tactics
Smokestick or Flash Vial first, then the crossbow from inside the smoke. Everyone Has a Price goes to whoever is about to land the killing blow. Close Call and Contingency keep him standing far longer than he should.
Bram Tallis
Tactics
Wins the first exchange, then loses his nerve. He runs rather than hides; a determined chase will catch him, and a cornered Bram talks rather than fights.
