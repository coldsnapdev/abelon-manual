Night 0: The Flight

Night of 27 Saeleth. Out of Tallowhaven after Alric's death, about ten miles on the road, a camp, and Kal.
At a glanceThe party leaves within the hour of the Shallow Tuber. One Survival check on the dark road, a choice between the Star and Raven and a roadside camp, and Kal discovered as a stowaway. They start a long rest around 2am, so Day 1 begins at about 10am.

Leaving

  • Out of the Furrows and onto the cart: Vernon and Mingyu in the traces. Tallowhaven has no watch, so nobody stops them at the east gate.
  • The danger is witnesses, not pursuit. Coll was grappled at the scene and saw them. Oskar is drinking at the Tuber under Suggestion. Nobody follows tonight, but by morning someone will know which way the cart went.

The road in the dark

  • Barely any moon, a loaded cart, nervous horses. Whoever drives makes one Wisdom (Survival) check, DC 10.
  • Success: about ten miles in three hours. Failure: a wheel goes into the ditch and costs an hour.

Where they stop

  • Brindle Hollow and the Star and Raven are about ten miles out. Mirelda's Coin means free beds and Seren is there, but it's the first place anyone from Tallowhaven would look.
  • Off the road past Brindle Hollow, a camp in the dark. Let them choose.

Kal

  • He's a stowaway, not a pursuer. He saw the party come out of the Tuber, read on their faces that they were leaving tonight, and climbed into the back of the cart among the provisions. He has heard everything they said on the road.
  • If they take the inn, he slips in behind them and is already in the common room.
  • Let Kaia choose when he's found: when someone unloads the provisions, or later, lifting something from a pack during a watch.
  • Both sides end up holding something over the other: they caught him in their cart, and he heard what they did tonight.
  • His spark-stone pendant (stolen from Edvar Crost) is dormant. It needs a live Aeronic source to attune.

The rest

  • Watches set, long rest from about 2am. Day 1 starts around 10am, which suits Brannoc's snare on the verge just as well.
