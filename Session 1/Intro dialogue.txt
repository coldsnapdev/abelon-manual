--OPENING MONOLOGUE--

Abelon sleeps. 

A rainy night on the western edge of the western continent is reluctantly giving way to another grey morning. 

In Brindle Hollow, birds are the first signs of life as their song joins the sounds of wind and rain. The small village is a mix of communities no bigger than a large family and dense woodland teaming with life both flora and fauna.

Somewhere in between the homesteads and the trees lies The Star and Raven Tavern. A small, welcoming inn that claims a small number of bedrooms, exquisite local ales, and the best atmosphere in the Western Reach.

Inside the tavern couldn't be more different to the drab and dreary world outside. Its low ceilings darkened by the roaring fire, smooth stone walls and scuffed floor are enough to tell you that this place has been here at least as long as the smaller trees surrounding it, perhaps some of the taller ones as well.

The strong smell of breakfast food and hot brewed drinks hints at the Star and Raven's reputation as the place to go to start your day. Indeed, despite the early hour, the barmaid has plenty of mouths to feed and mugs to fill.

Among them, sat alone at a table near the fire is a small, well-dressed Elf. Kaia, would you like to introduce your character?

Emerging from the basement, where the Tavern's modest bedrooms are located, emerge two rather-more-imposing figures. Similar, though not identical, it is the darker haired of the two Goliaths that emerges first. Kerry, would you like to introduce your character?

Following Jenok, the lighter haired of the two scans the room. Jord, would you like to introduce your character?

At that point, the door opens and in walks a remarkably tall Gnome. Mini, would you like to introduce your character?

The door opens again, this time heralding the arrival of a tall, lean figure. The Aasimar's pearlescent, shimmering skin, luminous eyes and unmistakable aura fill the doorway. Taz, would you like to introduce your character?

--Stillwater Shrine--

Every path in the village bends toward a ring of water at its centre, cart ruts and footlines converging like spokes on a wheel. No fence marks it off. Washing lines and woodpiles crowd right up to the stone edge. A brazier burns low in the middle of the pool, its smoke drifting up to join the thin trails rising from a couple of chimneys nearby.

--Ambush Site--

A wide, sunken ring of stone breaks the grassland, its rim worn and half collapsed in places. Sheep graze right up to the edge. A narrow stone bridge runs straight toward it, crossing a stream that curls around the site on two sides. Boulders sit scattered across the open ground, offering the only real cover between the bridge and the barrow itself.

--Aldermen's Hall — Exterior--

Stone below, timber above. The upper storey is a darker wood than the rest of the building, jutting out over a row of colonnaded pillars that shade the entrance. A wide set of steps leads up to the doors, worn smooth in a single track up the middle. Two plaques are mounted on the wall either side of the entrance, too far off to read from the street.

--Aldermen's Hall — Magistrate--
Rows of wooden benches face a low, red-draped dais at the far end of the room. Candle sconces line the walls, the only real light source given how narrow the windows are. Ledgers are stacked at the edges of the room, their corners worn. A single seat sits on the dais with a cushion in front of it, and a framed crest hangs on the wall above.
