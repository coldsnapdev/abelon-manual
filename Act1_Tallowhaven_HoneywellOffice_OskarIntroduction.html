Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Tallowhaven / Trademasters
Honeywell Office
Meeting Oskar Sternov — the Trademaster thread opens
Social encounter No combat Sent by Morra Leads to warehouse mission
Environment

The Honeywell building is a working steelworks on the edge of Tallowhaven's industrial quarter — a place that processes raw materials into usable sheets and ingots for shipping. The party hears it before they see it. Inside the ground floor: heat, hammering, the smell of hot metal, workers who do not look up because they are busy. The party must navigate through to find the stairs at the back.

The office above the floor is functional and slightly cramped. A window looks down onto the operation below. Ledgers, schedules, maps of supply routes, a desk that has seen years of use. Oskar is here because he likes being above the work. He finds it clarifying.

The floor Loud, hot, purposeful. Workers who do not acknowledge strangers. A world that runs without the party's involvement and will continue to do so. The contrast with the Shallow Tuber is immediate and deliberate.
The stairs At the back of the floor. A worker will point them there if asked. Nobody stops them.
The office Practical furniture, everything with a purpose. The window onto the floor below is the only architectural flourish. Oskar chose this building because it suits him.
The view The window looking down at the floor. Oskar glances at it occasionally during conversation. He is always aware of what is happening below.

Appearance

A man who has worked his way up through the actual business rather than inherited a position in it. Practical clothing, broad hands, the bearing of someone who has been doing this for twenty years and intends to do it for twenty more. He is not unfriendly. He is simply not interested in anything that does not move the work forward.

His manner
Morra's nameHe receives it with a brief nod. He trusts her judgement. That is enough to get the party in the door.
No small talkHe does not do what Alric does. He does not ask questions to build a picture. He has a problem, he has heard the party can help, he describes the problem. That is the meeting.
The briefTwo sentences. Warehouses on the south side, been empty for years, needed for festival storage in four days. People have moved in. He needs them out. No fuss. No bodies. Half now, half when it is done.
On the workersHe knows they are there. He does not know much about them as individuals. He has not been to see them himself — that is not his job. He describes them as squatters without particular malice. They are in the way of something he needs.
QuestionsIf the party asks questions he answers directly. He does not know how many people are there. He does not know how long they have been there. He knows the warehouses are needed and the deadline is real.
What he does not say
The human costHe has not thought about where the workers will go. That is not the question he is solving. The party will think about it. That is appropriate.
Encounter Beats
01 Through the floor ▼

The party enters the ground floor. It is loud and hot and nobody acknowledges them. A worker eventually points them toward the stairs when asked. The world here does not stop for strangers.

This beat is brief but worth playing. The sensory contrast with Tallowhaven's civic and commercial spaces is the point. The Trademasters operate at the level where things are actually made and moved.

02 The office — Oskar ▼

Oskar is at his desk when they arrive. He looks up, receives Morra's name, gestures to chairs or does not depending on how busy he looks. He gets to it.

He describes the problem in two sentences. Warehouses on the south side, empty for years, needed for festival storage in four days. People have moved in. He needs them out cleanly. Half now, half on completion. Any questions.

03 Questions and payment ▼

He answers questions directly and without elaboration. He does not know the details of who is there. He knows the deadline is real — the first delivery is locked in and the festival schedule does not move.

He pays the first half before they leave. He does not make a ceremony of it. A deal is a deal.

He glances at the floor below once during this conversation. He is always aware of what is happening down there.

04 The party leaves ▼

Oskar returns to his work before they are fully out the door. The meeting is over. The floor below continues. The party leaves through the same heat and noise they came through.

The job is simple as described. What they find at the warehouses is not what a simple job looks like.

DM Notes
Oskar vs AlricThe contrast is the point. Alric had breakfast, asked questions, paid at the table with a purse from his aide. Oskar described the job in two sentences and went back to work. Both are legitimate. Both are interested in results. The party is working out what kind of world Tallowhaven is and these two men are part of that education.
The floorDo not rush past it. A brief description of the heat and the noise and the workers who do not look up does more for establishing the Trademasters' world than any amount of exposition about what the guild does. The party is physically inside the operation before Oskar says a word.
The four day deadlineReal. The first delivery is locked in. Oskar is not dramatising it. If the party tries to negotiate more time he will tell them precisely why there is no more time. He is not being difficult. He is describing a supply chain.
