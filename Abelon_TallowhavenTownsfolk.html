Tallowhaven Townsfolk

Forty ready-made townsfolk for anyone the party stops to talk to. Each has a token and an entry in the Abelon Namer.
Using this listPick whoever suits the moment, play the one-line personality, and cross them off or promote them once the party learns their name. Their names stay hidden on the map until you reveal them with the Namer.
