Mission Reference — Act 1 / Courtesans Network / Second Mission
The Starling
Find Elbeth Sable before the deadline — the situation is not what it appears
Investigation then combatLevel 3 partyCombat in tight quarters — deadlyConsequence for Merchants — opaque to party

Seva asks the party to find and rescue Elbeth Sable, missing for two days after a threatening note. The trail runs through a panicking young delegate who sent the note himself and lost control of it, to a fixer who arranged the original job, to a stables where Elbeth is being held by opportunists who bit off more than they could chew. Elbeth walks out under her own power. What the party never learns is that this whole affair quietly costs Alric a concession he was counting on.

At a glance
Part 1 — Investigation
Seva's brief: unusually direct -- this is personal, not professional. Elbeth is missing; a threatening note points to Emlyn Hale of the Driftwood delegation.
Emlyn Hale: sent the note himself to sound powerful, then the hired muscle overstepped and took Elbeth for real. He's now being extorted by everyone involved and cracks fast under any real pressure.
Oakstrong: the fixer who arranged the original job, self-named with complete sincerity. Gives up the address once he realises the situation has escalated past what he agreed to facilitate.
The hidden consequence: Emlyn returns to his delegation duties shaken and unreliable on a clause Alric needed -- Alric notices and looks at the Courtesans. The party never sees this connection.
Part 2 — The Coppergate Stables
Setup: tight quarters, one real entrance, a roof ladder for a careful approach. Drace's crew isn't expecting a rescue party.
Drace: an opportunist, shrewder than his crew -- will negotiate and walk away if he's clearly losing, no ideological commitment to any of this
Elbeth: injured but coherent, furious, and walks out under her own power. Has spent two days listening and has a full picture of the operation to hand Seva.
Easter egg: Harwick wears a Broken Wing emblem -- not an operative, just resonated with the symbol. Third context the party may have seen it in. Means nothing yet.
Location Descriptions
The Anchor and Reed

The respectable inn where the Driftwood delegation is quartered for festival week -- not hard to find, and Emlyn hasn't left his room in two days.

The Tallow and Grain

A working quarter establishment busy enough that a conversation doesn't stand out -- where Oakstrong conducts business.

The Coppergate Stables

A single-storey working stables on a quiet street. The stable yard has hay bales, a horse pen, and enough daily traffic that the building doesn't stand out. Inside: an internal staircase to a small mezzanine balcony, and an external roof ladder offering a careful party an unexpected angle in. Elbeth is held in the sleeping area at the rear of the ground floor.

NPCs
Seva Sable The employer — controlled, genuinely worried, gives the starting point
▼
Personality points

More direct than usual -- composure intact, but the party will notice this isn't a professional assessment. Elbeth matters to her personally; she's not performing distress, but she's not performing calm either.

Goals for this encounter

Find Elbeth, get her out safely. Explicitly does not want the delegation touched or a scene made during festival week.

What she knows

Describes the threatening note without showing it -- something about a starling coming home in fewer feathers. Believes it's a kidnapping; she's not wrong, just not seeing the whole picture. Points the party at Emlyn Hale and the Anchor and Reed.

What she keeps secret

Will handle Emlyn Hale herself after the festival -- not loud, not shared with the party. Afterward, asks one question about what he was like when they found him, and files the answer away.

Emlyn Hale Driftwood delegation member — panicking, out of his depth, will tell everything
▼
Visual description

A young man of good family whose respectable position doesn't match his private habits -- posh, well connected, catastrophically out of his depth. Hasn't left his room in two days, and looks terrible.

Goals for this encounter

Sent the threatening note himself to sound powerful -- the hired muscle was only meant to make a vague point. They took Elbeth for real instead and are now extorting him too.

What he knows

The note, the muscle, the intermediary, and how badly it all went. Gives up Oakstrong's name once pressed.

What he keeps secret

Doesn't know Elbeth's location -- genuinely, not evasion. The intermediary handles that.

Personality points

Stonewalls at first (DC 13 Persuasion or Intimidation breaks it), then talks and doesn't stop. Frightened, slightly pathetic, genuinely relieved someone's trying to fix it -- may cry. The party will almost feel sorry for him.

Oakstrong Fixer — self-named, professional pride, knows the address
▼
Visual description

Operates in the working quarter, chose the name himself relatively recently, says it with complete sincerity.

Goals for this encounter

Protect his professional reputation -- won't give up a client's location easily on principle, but is pragmatic underneath it.

What he knows

The address. Drace has escalated far beyond what Oakstrong agreed to facilitate, and he has his own exposure now.

Personality points

DC 14 Persuasion, a reasonable bribe, or an unappealing enough alternative gets the address -- he gives it as a sensible business decision, not a capitulation. Tells the party's backs, as they leave, that whatever happens in there is nothing to do with him. He means it.

Drace Leader — opportunist, shrewder than the others, will negotiate if losing
▼
Goals for this encounter

Saw a frightened young man with money and made a calculation that was correct about the leverage, wrong about who the network might send.

Personality points

Shrewd, not sophisticated. Directs the others efficiently, uses the room's tight space to his advantage. No ideological commitment -- if the fight turns against him, he offers to walk away, practically, not desperately.

Combat

AC 16, HP 78. Multiattack (two attacks): Shortsword +7 (1d6+4 slashing) or Hand crossbow +7 (1d6+4 piercing, 30/120ft). Cunning Action. Evasion (no damage instead of half on a successful Dex save). Uncanny Dodge (reaction: halves the damage of one attack that hits him). Parry (reaction, on a melee attacker he can see hitting him: +3 AC against that attack, can turn a hit into a miss).

Sot, Harwick & Brenna Drace's crew
▼
Sot

Follows Drace, no particular investment beyond pay -- breaks if Drace breaks. AC 14, HP 42. Multiattack (two attacks): Handaxe +5 (1d6+3 slashing) melee or thrown (20/60ft).

Harwick

Wears a Broken Wing emblem (patch, pin, cloth at the wrist) -- not an operative, just found the symbol and it resonated. Here because Drace pays; the two things are unconnected in his mind. AC 13, HP 45. Multiattack (two attacks): Warhammer +5 (1d8+3 bludgeoning).

Brenna

The most dangerous of the three after Drace -- more capable, less predictable, doesn't break easily. Fights until dropped rather than surrendering if she's the last one standing. AC 16, HP 65. Multiattack (two attacks): Shortsword +6 (1d6+4 slashing), plus bonus action off-hand dagger +6 (1d4+4 piercing). Riposte (reaction: if a creature misses her with a melee attack, she makes one melee weapon attack against that creature).

Elbeth Sable The one being rescued — injured, furious, has been paying attention
▼
Visual description

Bruised, injured, coherent -- not frightened. Angry in the specific way of someone who's used two days productively.

What she knows

Has been listening for two days -- a detailed picture of Drace's operation, habits, and relationships, which she passes to Seva. Not relevant to the party's immediate task, but useful to Seva later.

Personality points

Walks out under her own power and makes a point of it -- doesn't want to be carried or supported unless she specifically asks. Says very little on the way back, saving it for Seva. On Emlyn: a sound that isn't quite a laugh. Zero sympathy -- she knew exactly what kind of person he was before any of this started.

Encounter Beats
I1 Seva's brief ▼

The party finds themselves upstairs at the private table, Mira's work already done. Seva gets to the point faster than usual -- no patience for preamble when someone she cares about is missing. She describes the note, names Emlyn Hale and the Anchor and Reed, pays half upfront, and makes clear that “done” means Elbeth safe, not merely located.

I2 Finding Emlyn ▼

The Anchor and Reed isn't hard to find. Emlyn opens his door looking terrible, visibly calculating whether these visitors are good or bad news. He tries to stonewall and isn't good at it -- once real pressure lands, he tells the party everything, gives up Oakstrong's name and the Tallow and Grain, and apologises more than once. He genuinely doesn't know where Elbeth is.

I3 Finding Oakstrong ▼

The Tallow and Grain is busy enough that nothing stands out. Oakstrong introduces himself without any sense that the name needs explaining, and resists giving up the address on professional grounds -- but not for long, since Drace's escalation has left him exposed too. He gives the address with the dignity of a pragmatic decision, and mentions to the party's backs, as they leave, that whatever happens next is nothing to do with him.

C1 The approach ▼

The party has the address. A careful approach (Stealth DC 13) lets them reach the door unheard and choose when the fight starts; a failed check gives Drace a round of preparation. There's no back exit -- a room with a door, and the party standing at it.

C2 The fight ▼

Four against the party in close quarters, where flanking is hard and going down is dangerous. This crew is a genuine match for the party, not a speed bump -- Drace directs efficiently and knows the room better than the party does, and Brenna's the sharpest edge, but Sot and Harwick are past the point of folding on the first hit too. Harwick's Broken Wing emblem is visible if a player looks closely -- don't draw attention to it, let it be found. If the fight turns clearly against him, Drace offers to walk away, practically, and takes Sot and Harwick with him if the party accepts. Brenna decides for herself.

C3 Finding Elbeth ▼
Elbeth recovered, thugs dealt withSeva pays the second half after receiving Elbeth in private, asks her one question about Emlyn, and files the answer. Something will happen to him after the festival -- quietly, and the party isn't told what.
Elbeth recovered, Drace walked awaySeva pays the second half and simply notes Drace is still out there -- not a criticism. She'll deal with it herself, in time.
Deadline missed but Elbeth recoveredPaid, and grateful Elbeth is safe -- but the missed deadline had consequences she doesn't share with the party.
Elbeth not recoveredSeva goes very still, thanks them for trying, and handles it herself. The relationship doesn't close, but it's different now -- she trusted them with something that mattered and it didn't work.

She's in the back room, and looks at whoever opens the door with the expression of someone who's been waiting and isn't going to make it easy by being grateful. She stands, walks to the door, and goes with the party under her own power.

DM Notes
Difficulty, deliberately raisedFour combatants, same as the plan, but built to actually threaten a party that's been handling fights well -- Uncanny Dodge and Parry on Drace, a genuine off-hand attack and Riposte on Brenna, and Sot and Harwick hitting hard enough that ignoring them is a mistake. Don't pull punches on their tactics to compensate; the room and the numbers are the leash, not the DM's mercy.
Emlyn Hale's fateSeva deals with him after the festival -- not physically, she's not that crude, but the leverage she already held over him now comes with the knowledge of what he did. He becomes notably noncommittal about a clause in the Driftwood deal; Alric notices. The party never connects it. That's correct.
Elbeth walking outDon't soften this. She's injured and walks out under her own power -- a statement about who she is and what the Network is. She's not going to perform vulnerability for the party's benefit.
The Broken Wing emblemDon't mention it in narration unless a player specifically looks at Harwick. If asked, he doesn't explain it -- personal, not something he discusses with people trying to hit him. The observation joins a pattern that pays off in Act 2.
OakstrongPlay him with complete sincerity. He chose the name and stands by it -- the comedy is in how seriously he takes himself, not in the party laughing at him.
