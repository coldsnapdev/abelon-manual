Catacombs, Floor 1: The Door
An earthen antechamber, a door that opens to a diagram, and the first burial chamber
Exploration puzzleHarmless failureNo combatLevel 3–4 party

The way down starts at a small hatch inside the shrinekeeper's outhouse and office. A ladder drops into an excavated earthen room with nothing in it except a single door. The door opens to a diagram in the shrinekeepers' ledger, which the party is carrying. Beyond it, a low burial chamber holds the first of Adelina Thornecroft's torn pages, lying on the floor, and two staircases down to a blind choice. Nothing on this floor can hurt anyone. Its job is to establish that the dungeon answers to attention, and to hand over the first page of the Rot notes before anyone has seen any Rot.

At a glance
Part 1: The Antechamber and Door
Access: Hatch in the shrinekeeper's outhouse and office, then a ladder down. Earth on every side, one door, nothing else.
The door: Five raised studs set in a diamond: four corners and a centre point. Aeron's Spark, the diamond within concentric circles, is carved above them.
The key: The ledger's door page shows the Spark with six numbered marks. Trace the outline from the top corner, around all four corners and back to the top, then press the centre.
Failure: Harmless. Nothing happens and the party tries again. This is a low-friction gate, not a trial.
Three hands on the page: Adelina's symbol, the numbers added by the adventurer who solved it first, and Otho's marginal "Door?" from his own attempt to work it out.
Part 2: The First Chamber
First page: One of Adelina's pages lies on the flagstones in plain view. Any look around finds it, with no roll.
Crates: Old keepers' storage along one wall. Clutter and atmosphere, nothing of value.
The descent: Two staircases down, chosen blind. Each lands at a different arrival point on floor 2.
Tone: Torchlit, still, readable and no longer welcoming. The shrine above is human and tired. This is the first place that feels old.
Location Descriptions
The hatch and ladder

A small hatch in the floor of the shrinekeeper's outhouse and office. The ladder is plain and well kept. Aldous's separate hatch holds only his personal trinkets.

The earthen antechamber

An excavated space, square and bare, packed earth on every side. The air is cool and a little stale. The only feature is the door, so everything the party does here is about the door.

The door

Heavy and plain, set flush into the earth. Five raised studs form a diamond at chest height, with Aeron's Spark carved above them. Pressing a stud makes it sink slightly and click. A wrong order does nothing visible and the studs reset after a moment. The door swings inward once the full sequence is accepted.

The first chamber

A low vaulted burial chamber of rough stone, entered down a few steps from the door. Crates stand against one wall and a stepped threshold leads on. Dust lies thick and undisturbed apart from the middle of the floor.

Loot

No roll: a torn, yellowed page lies on the flagstones in the middle of the room. It is the first of Adelina's pages and opens with her diary entry before it moves to "What it is."

Optional moment: the torn edge fits the stubs in the ledger. Anyone who holds the page to the binding sees it match.

The two staircases

A pair of staircases leads down. Neither shows anything of what is below. Open floor 2 at the arrival point that matches the staircase the party takes, so the choice stays blind.

Encounter Beats
1 The hatch and the ladder ▼

The party arrives at the shrine knowing the dungeon exists and carrying the ledger. The way down is a small hatch in the outhouse and office, the one place Aldous is seen entering earlier. Keep the descent short and quiet. The ladder is well maintained, which tells the party that someone has used this way for generations.

Costin, if he is present, will not go down and will not help beyond pointing at the ledger by its full title, "Everbloom Shrine, Ledger, curated by Adelina Thornecroft."

2 The door ▼

Give the party a moment in the bare earthen room before the door gets any attention. Let them notice that there is nothing else here. Then let them find the studs and the carved Spark.

The door page in the ledger carries all three hands. Read them in order if the party asks: Adelina's symbol is the diagram, the numbers are a different hand, and the "Door?" beside it is Otho's. The party can solve it by tracing the diamond outline and pressing the centre. Let players who work it out from the numbers feel clever, and let players who never connect the page to the studs try combinations until something works. The door has no failure state.

SolvedThe door swings inward onto the first chamber.
Stuck for a long timeGive the Insight or Investigation DC 10 hint before the scene stalls.
3 The first chamber ▼

Describe the chamber before anything else: low roof, crates, dust, the sense of a place that was kept and then left. Let the page on the floor be the first thing a player describes themselves doing. A looking-around question gets it at once.

Read the opening of the page aloud if a player reads it. It begins with Adelina's diary entry and moves into "What it is." Hand the page over as a physical or on-screen prop and let the players decide who keeps it.

4 The blind choice ▼

Two staircases lead down. Ask which one the party takes and open floor 2 at the matching arrival point. Do not describe floor 2 before they commit.

DM Notes
Nothing here hurts.Floor 1 is deliberately safe. Its purpose is to teach the party that the ledger is a tool, that pages are worth picking up, and that the dungeon rewards looking. Keep the pace relaxed.
The door stays simple.The five-point diamond is the whole puzzle. The numbers on the page exist so the solution is written down, and nobody has to remember a sequence. Do not add a time limit, a trap or a second layer.
The adventurer is not on this floor.A previous visitor solved the door and went through ahead of the party. The only trace they left here is the numbers written on the ledger page. Do not stage them in this scene.
The first page.Pages are handed out in order whichever one the party finds. The first one found is always the diary entry and "What it is." This floor has one page, floor 2 has two and floor 3 has two. The adventurer's note on floor 4 stays fixed to his body and sits outside that order.
Map.Floor 1 is a single scene with two staircase exits. Add a hidden GM note to the scene listing the page and the two descents.
