Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Tallowhaven / Third Missions
The Assassination Paths
The night before the signing. One target or two, each alone, each ready.
Combat — conditionalMoral weight: extremeFollows Evening InvitationsFeeds the signing

This page picks up after the seven o'clock meetings on the Evening Invitations page. Alric asked for Seva. Seva asked for Oskar. Nobody asked the party for Alric, because Oskar is sending Bram instead. If the party took a job, they go to the target tonight. Each target is alone and fights alone: Seva stays in the private area at the Amber Cup with Mira looking in now and then, Oskar is alone at Honeywell once Bram has gone, Alric stays in the private area at the Shallow Tuber, and Huw is unconscious in Serastra's Rest. The fight is five on one if the party stays together, and genuinely dangerous if they split.

At a glance
Seva runs, Oskar doesn't: Seva leaves through the nearest exit below 41 HP, so killing her means cutting off the exits first. Oskar fights until he drops, and Not Today makes the first apparent kill a false one.
Neither is surprised: Seva can't be surprised at all. Oskar suspected something was coming. Neither begs, and both talk as though they are still winning.
Oskar is briefing Bram when they arrive: a stealthy party can overhear the job they were never offered. Bram then leaves for Alric, and Oskar is alone.
Alric is an unlikely target, not an impossible one: a party that goes for him will notice Bram trying the same thing, and can meet him before or during his attempt. Otherwise Bram fails off camera.

Location Descriptions
The Amber Cup (Seva)

Busy all evening. Seva stays in the private area where she met the party, off the main room. Mira stops by every so often; a party paying attention can tell when she is there and when she isn't, and plan around it. The crowd and the noise cover a short fight but not a long one. Seva knows every way out of the building, and the party has to find them; cutting off the exits before the fight turns is what makes a kill possible.

Honeywell, after hours (Oskar)

The forge floor below the Honeywell office, the forge banked and still hot. When the party arrives, Oskar is briefing Bram. Once Bram leaves for the Shallow Tuber, Oskar is alone; there are no apprentices tonight. He keeps the forge at his back. Anyone thrown into the open forge takes extra fire damage, and Banked Coals only works within 10 feet of it.

The Shallow Tuber (Alric)

Busy all evening. Alric stays in the private area where he met the party, having sent his guards away for a busy night, which is exactly why Bram thinks tonight is the night. Huw is not with him.

Honeywell: the briefing
If the party is stealthy

Dexterity (Stealth) against Oskar's passive Perception 11 and Bram's 13 lets them overhear Oskar giving Bram the job he would have given the party, had they ever got close enough to him. If they are spotted, Bram leaves anyway: Alric is his job tonight, and Oskar sends him.

What Oskar says (draft): Alric hired outsiders to break the Driftwood road, and that cost people Oskar answers to more than Alric will ever know. Tomorrow's signing hands Alric the city. Tonight he is at the Shallow Tuber, in the private area, with his guards sent away. Do it quietly, leave nothing that leads back to Honeywell, and don't come back here tonight. Oskar never names the people he answers to, and Bram doesn't ask.

Seva SableTarget if the party sided with Alric
▼
When they arrive

Still in the private area at the Amber Cup, unhurried, already aware. If some of the party were at her seven o'clock meeting, she has had time to wonder where the others went. She works out why they've come in about two seconds and shows nothing.

How she fights

Words first: Whispered Doubt on the strongest attacker, aimed at whoever they trust most, and Danzel first if he is there. Then an attack and a Hide or Disengage every turn. She leaves at half health.

Mira

Stops by every so often and does not fight. A watchful party knows when she is there. If Seva is killed, Mira is the first to see what's left.

Oskar SternovTarget if the party sided with Seva
▼
When they arrive

Briefing Bram at the forge. Once Bram has gone he goes back to work, sleeves rolled, hammer in hand. When the party steps in, he looks up, sees who it is and how many, and puts the next piece back in the fire. He was expecting someone tonight, just not them.

How he fights

Keeps the forge at his back and the party between him and the door. Grapples small, quick characters and hurls them into their friends or the coals. Goes for whoever hurts him most. Doesn't flee.

Why he's alone

He has just sent Bram after Alric, which is exactly why he is exposed tonight. No apprentices are working.

DM Notes
Five on one is the pointIf the party wins, it's because they outnumbered their target, not because they were better. Prepared for This and Not Today are there to stop it ending in a round, so the party has time to notice what they're doing.
If they splitTwo or three on one is genuinely dangerous. Let them live with that choice rather than softening it.
Preparation countsA party that scouts, cuts off exits or catches a target off guard should feel the benefit, especially against Seva.
Kept away counts as a successAs at the meetings, a leader kept away from the signing counts as a success for whoever hired the party, even if they survive.
The scenarios are a first draftThe outcome notes and Oskar's briefing follow the Evening Invitations and Guild Leaders designs; the crossover details and the briefing wording are new and worth reading before the session.
