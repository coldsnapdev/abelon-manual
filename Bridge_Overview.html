The Road to Crossroads

The Bridging Act between Acts 1 and 2: about twelve days and three hundred miles east from Tallowhaven, day by day and night by night.
ShapeAbout 12 days on the road, about 300 miles, at the cart's pace. Five travellers and two horses (Vernon and Mingyu) means the cart sets the speed, which is roughly walking pace. Towns at Farrowfield (Day 6) and Greenrest (Day 9). The party carries some provisions, and the cart's four days are deliberately short so they must restock.
The rule of the roadDaytime encounters are relatively easy to overcome, and everyone the party talks to has at least one useful fact (from the pool below). The real threats come at night. No Rot Beasts before Crossroads: the Rot isn't concentrated enough out here to sustain them. Beasts become ordinary enemies on the way to Shatterspeak.
The calendarThey leave on the night of 27 Saeleth. Day 12 is 39 Saeleth, and Halfbright is 40 Saeleth, so they reach Crossroads on the eve of the festival: a city full of unrest, the Lamplighters stretched thin, on the one night of the season everyone is out in the streets.

The fact pool

Each NPC has a DC: 10 chatty, 13 wary, 16 guarded. A failed check gives a Common fact; meeting the DC gives a Useful one; beating it by 5 or more gives a Valuable one. Pick the best-fitting fact and cross it off. All of them are true. Facts 7 and 8 are the strongest: save them for a good roll.

Carried along the road

Mirelda's Cointhe Hearthkeepers' Accord

Shown to any innkeeper, it means free beds for the bearer and their company: beds only, food and drink paid as usual. Danzel recognises it straight away. It works at the Star and Raven, the Plough and Lantern, and in Greenrest.

Thalen's shrineoptional

Thalen (Gate) can have a small shrine at a gate or bridge later in the journey.

Loose ends

Kal at Fennick's cartFennick's stock still includes Wren's scrolls. If Wren has left, Kal needs something on the ledge instead. That depends on Kal's class.
An Aeronic sourceKal's spark-stone and the party's ring both need a live Aeronic source to wake. If the party skips Everbloom, the only known one is behind them (the catacomb base; Stillwater is barred). Neither relic wakes on this road unless a source is placed somewhere east.
If they skip EverbloomThe Festival Meeting and the Catacombs go unplayed, and the Tallowhaven saga ends with the flight rather than a climax. That's acceptable; it just means the road has to carry the next stretch on its own.
