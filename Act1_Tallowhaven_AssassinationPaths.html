Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Tallowhaven / Third Missions
The Assassination Paths
Seven o'clock. Three private rooms. The party arrives too late for a briefing — but in time for a choice.
Combat — conditional Moral weight: extreme Follows Evening Invitations Feeds Festival Finale
How these scenes workThe party was invited to meet with one faction leader at seven o'clock. The other two faction leaders are also in private rooms at seven o'clock — they had their own meetings to hold. The party can only attend one briefing. But they know where the other two are. If they go to a target rather than their own faction leader, the target reads the room almost immediately. They are not expecting the party. The party is not there for a briefing. What they are there for becomes clear within moments. The party then chooses: kill, or warn. Both paths lead to the Festival Finale, but through different doors.
When this appliesThe party attended Alric's briefing and chose to act against Seva — or they skipped the briefing entirely and came here. Seva is in a private room above the Amber Cup. She chose this room because she controls it. That was wise thinking for every scenario except this one.
Location — Private Room, The Amber Cup

A private room above the main floor of the Amber Cup. Comfortable rather than grand — a round table, three chairs, candles, a decanter of wine that has not been touched. The door opens inward. There is one window overlooking the street below, too narrow to exit through quickly.

Mira is outside the door in the corridor when the party arrives. She recognises them. She lets them in without question — they are known faces. She steps in behind them.

Seva is seated. She looks up when the door opens, expecting her next appointment. Then she reads the room. It takes her approximately two seconds. She does not show what she concludes.

The roomSmall, enclosed. The table limits movement. Two exits — the door and the window. Neither is fast. Mira controls the door from behind the party.
LightingThree candles on the table. Warm, low. Enough to see clearly. Nothing dramatic about it.
NoiseThe Amber Cup is busy below. Festival week. Music, conversation, the clink of glasses. A fight in this room would not be heard immediately.
MiraBehind the party. Between them and the door. She will not draw a weapon until the situation is clear — but the moment it is clear, she will.
The Moment of Realisation

Seva looks at the party for a moment. Then she looks at the door — where Mira is standing. Then back at the party. She reaches for nothing. She does not stand.

Seva — quietly, to the roomClose the door, Mira.

A pause. When Mira has done it:

SevaYou are not here for a briefing. So. What are you here for?

She asks it directly. She already knows the answer. She wants to hear them say it — partly to understand exactly what they have decided, partly because she is buying the two seconds it takes Mira to position herself. She is entirely calm. This is not the first dangerous room she has been in.

Path A — Kill Seva
Kill path — Seva and Mira fight to the end ▼

The fight happens in a small room with a busy tavern below. It is over quickly — two against a full party in a confined space, neither of them willing to flee. When it is done the party has killed the leader of the Courtesans Network in a private room above the Amber Cup on festival night.

Getting out requires passing through the main floor of the Amber Cup. Morra will be behind the bar. She will look at them. She will know something happened upstairs. She will not stop them. She will remember everything.

Path B — Warn Seva
Warn path — Seva listens, assesses, and responds ▼

The party tells her they were briefed by Alric to remove her. She listens without expression. When they finish she is quiet for a moment — not processing the information, she had already anticipated something like this — but deciding what the party is worth.

Seva — after a pauseAlric is more direct than I gave him credit for. Thank you for telling me.

She asks two questions: what exactly did Alric say, and what does the party want in return. She does not offer more than she is asked for unless the party asks. If they ask for coin she pays — a purse produced from somewhere on her person without ceremony. If they ask for nothing she files that too.

She tells them she will need them at the meeting tomorrow. Standing with the Courtesans. She expects them to be there. She says it as a statement of fact rather than a request. Whether the party agrees is their choice — she does not labour the point.

Seva — on the meetingCome tomorrow. Stand where I can see you. That is all I am asking.

When this appliesThe party attended Oskar's briefing and chose to act against Alric — or they skipped the briefing entirely and came here. Alric is in a private room at the Shallow Tuber. He reserved it. He is used to being the one who sets the terms of a meeting. That assumption is about to become a liability.
Location — Private Room, The Shallow Tuber

A private dining room at the back of the Shallow Tuber. Clean tablecloths, a meal that has not been touched, candles on a sideboard. The room is comfortable and contained — Alric uses it precisely because it is quiet and controlled.

Huw opens the door. He recognises the party immediately. He steps back to admit them, his expression professionally neutral. He moves to one side as they enter, placing himself between the party and the exit.

Alric is at the table. He looks up expecting the beginning of a conversation he has carefully prepared. Then he reads the room. It takes him slightly longer than Seva — he is a systems thinker, not an instinct reader — but he gets there.

The roomRectangular, one door, no window large enough to matter. The table runs most of the length. Movement around it is limited.
HuwBy the door. The party walked past him to get in. He is between them and the exit. He has not yet drawn anything.
The mealUntouched. Alric does not eat at meetings. He ordered it because it signals that this is a relaxed conversation between people with aligned interests. That signal has expired.
NoiseThe Shallow Tuber is quieter than the Amber Cup — a respectable establishment on a festival evening. There are other diners. The private room is soundproofed enough to require raised voices to be heard outside.
The Moment of Realisation

Alric looks at the party. He looks at Huw. He sets down the papers he was holding.

Alric — carefullyYou are not here to discuss what I asked you to discuss.

He does not phrase it as a question. He is updating his model of the situation in real time and he does not want to waste words while he does it. After a moment:

AlricI want to understand what has brought you to this room instead of the other one. Before anything else happens.

He is buying time. He is also genuinely trying to understand — he thinks in systems and he wants to know which system failed. Whether the party explains or moves is their choice. Either way Huw is watching.

The one thing
He is angrier than he has ever been. Not at the party exactly — at the situation, at the failure of his own planning, at whatever betrayal brought them here. That anger makes him more dangerous than his stats suggest and less predictable than usual.

Kill path — Alric and Huw fight to the end ▼

Two against a full party in a private dining room. Huw is the more capable fighter but he is protecting rather than attacking, which limits him. Alric fights with anger rather than skill. The fight is unlikely to last long.

The party tells him Oskar briefed them to remove him. He listens with the expression of someone who is not particularly surprised. He asks one question: what exactly did Oskar say. He listens to the answer without interrupting.

Location — Honeywell Office, After Hours

The Honeywell office above the forge floor. The forge below is banked for the night but still warm — the heat rises through the boards. The office is functional: a desk with years of use, ledgers, supply route maps, a window looking down at the dark floor below.

Tav is on the stairs when the party arrives. He recognises them — he has seen them at the Festival Finale briefings. He is not unfriendly but he is watchful. He shows them up without being asked.

Oskar is at his desk. He does not look up immediately when they enter. When he does, he reads them in the way he reads most things — quickly, without showing it. He sets down his pen.

The officeCramped and functional. The desk dominates. Movement around it is awkward. The window onto the forge floor below is glass — it can be broken but not quietly.
The stairsThe only exit. Tav came up behind the party. He is now at the top of the stairs, blocking the descent.
The forge floorEmpty and dark below. If the window goes, it is a fifteen-foot drop onto cold stone. Nobody is fleeing that way.
NoiseThe forge district is quieter at night than the festival quarter. A fight in this office would be contained. Nobody is coming.
The Moment of Realisation

Oskar looks at the party. He looks at Tav. He does not reach for anything. He stands.

Oskar — without inflectionYou are not here about the meeting.

He does not ask it. A pause. He looks at each of them in turn, the way he looked at the road crew on the Driftwood checkpoint — assessing the situation for what it is rather than what he would prefer it to be.

OskarThen say what you have come to say.

He crosses his arms. He is not performing calm — he is actually calm. He has been in difficult rooms before. He knows there are two of them and more of the party. He has already concluded that running is not an option and has moved past it.
