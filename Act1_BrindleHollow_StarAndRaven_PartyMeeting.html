Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Brindle Hollow
The Star and Raven
Session one opening — the party meets, and receives the job
Social encounterNo combatSession Zero / One openingJob offered by Morfudd Pryce

The party gathers at a tavern in Brindle Hollow on a wet evening, meets each other, and is approached by Morfudd Pryce, a local Aeronist priest whose seeding ceremony was disrupted by a wandering former paladin named Marek Talan. He offers to pay for an escort to take Marek to Tallowhaven to face the magistrate. The party accepts, and is told to collect Marek from the Stillwater Shrine in the morning.

At a glance
Purpose: the party meets, the world is established, a job is offered. Let it breathe — nothing here needs to seed toward later acts, it just needs to feel real.
The job: escort Marek Talan (under citizen's arrest) to the magistrate in Tallowhaven. Payment on completion; Morfudd can't come himself.
Morfudd's account: accurate in its facts, uncharitable in its framing — Marek disrupted a ritual sacrifice, non-violently, and left when asked.
Optional texture: Nesta (barkeep), Coll (farmer), Reed (traveller from Tallowhaven), Seren (ancient card player) all reward curiosity but none of it is required
Location Descriptions
The Star and Raven

Low ceilings, heavy beams darkened by years of smoke, stone walls, worn wooden floors. Candles and a single fireplace provide most of the light. It smells of woodsmoke and something cooking in the back. Outside, a rainy evening streaks the small windows. The room isn't full, but it's occupied — a handful of regulars, a traveller, two dwarves after a shift, and an elf in the corner doing well at cards.

NPCs
Nesta Barkeep — warm, observant, knows everything
▼
Visual description

Mid-forties, hair pulled back practically, an apron that's seen better days — the face of someone who's seen everything a small village can produce and stayed fond of it anyway.

Goals for this encounter

Will mention Morfudd's need for capable people if the party seems at a loose end, or if asked directly. Doesn't oversell it.

What she knows

Everything that's happened in Brindle Hollow for twenty years — won't volunteer it, but answers honestly if asked.

Personality points

Notices the party before they notice her. Welcoming without being effusive.

Morfudd Pryce Aeronist priest — the employer
▼
Visual description

Middle-aged, thin, slightly officious. Well-kept dark robes with green and blue embroidery, receding hair, a neatly trimmed beard, and the expression of a man whose ceremony was ruined by a one-handed paladin making a point about a goat.

Goals for this encounter

Hire the party to escort Marek Talan to the magistrate in Tallowhaven. He can't do it himself — he has the shrine to manage.

What he knows

Describes Marek as a troublemaker and disruptor — accurate in the facts, uncharitable in the framing. Genuinely believes Marek was wrong.

What he keeps secret

Nothing deliberately — he's slightly embarrassed to be seeking help in a tavern, but has run out of better options.

Personality points

Pompous rather than rude. Considers himself a person of standing and is accustomed to being taken seriously. Resistant but not immovable on payment — he's spending his own money and feels the pinch.

Seren Elf — Crowns and Crows, corner table (optional)
▼
Visual description

Quietly androgynous, ageless in a way that makes it impossible to guess two hundred years old or five hundred. Fine features, unhurried expression, a hand of cards visible at the edge of the frame.

Goals for this encounter

None — entirely optional. Doesn't approach the party; available if they investigate.

What they know

Has been in Brindle Hollow a long time. Knows about the shrine, Morfudd, and the general rhythm of the place — won't volunteer much, but answers thoughtfully.

Personality points

Delighted rather than annoyed if a player wins at cards — someone interesting finally sat down. Accepts losing with quiet grace and buys the loser a drink either way.

Background patrons Coll, Reed, Borek & Yael
▼
Coll

Local farmer, late fifties — more sympathetic to Marek than he'd admit publicly, if drawn out.

Reed

A traveller from Tallowhaven, deliberately unremarkable, alert eyes. Can describe the city right now: busy with festival prep, slightly tense, money moving in ways not everyone's comfortable with.

Borek and Yael

Two dwarves after a shift — not plot relevant, just texture establishing this as a mixed, lived-in world.

Encounter Beats
1 Arrival ▼

The party arrives at the Star and Raven on a wet evening. The room is warm, Nesta's behind the bar, the fire is going. Let players introduce their characters to each other — don't force it. Give them things to interact with (the bar, the fire, the dwarves, Seren's game) and let introductions happen naturally.

2 The world in miniature ▼

Purely optional engagement. Nesta gives a warm, practical read on Brindle Hollow; Coll gives a local's perspective; Reed gives a glimpse of Tallowhaven before they arrive; Seren gives something older and harder to read. None of it is required. All of it rewards curiosity.

3 Morfudd arrives ▼

Morfudd enters and introduces himself with his full name, finding the party either through Nesta's recommendation or by identifying them as capable-looking strangers. He explains the situation: Marek disrupted his seeding ceremony, he made a citizen's arrest, and he needs an escort to Tallowhaven. He's slightly embarrassed to be doing this in a tavern, but keeps his dignity. He pays on completion — he can't come himself.

4 Questions and negotiation ▼

Morfudd answers questions honestly, with his characteristic slight pomposity. His account of Marek is telling: a man who made arguments rather than threats, disrupted rather than damaged, and left when asked. The party may draw their own conclusions about whether this required an arrest.

If they push on payment, he's resistant but not immovable — a persuasive case gets a small concession.

5 The job is accepted ▼

Morfudd tells them Marek is being held at the Stillwater Shrine, where they'll begin in the morning. He leaves like a man who's solved a problem and would rather not think about it further. The rest of the evening is the party's — the room's still warm, the rain's still outside, Seren's still at the card table.

DM Notes
Session one toneThis scene sets the register for everything that follows. Grounded, unhurried, human. Nobody is special yet — the world is simply in front of them and they're deciding what to do with it.
Crowns and CrowsIf players actually play the card game with Seren, run it as Insight vs Deception, contested. Seren has a high Deception modifier reflecting centuries of practice. Winning is genuinely satisfying; losing is graceful. Either way, Seren buys a drink and conversation opens.
Morfudd's pomposityPlay it straight — not a villain, not comic relief. A man of modest local standing whose ceremony was disrupted, who believes he's entirely in the right. He probably is, technically. That's part of what makes Marek interesting.
No need to seed forwardNothing in this scene needs to point toward Act 2 or 3. The world is established simply by being itself — trust that.
