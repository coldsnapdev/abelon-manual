Combat Reference — The Starling

Drace's crew at Coppergate Stables — surprise likely — Level 3 party — Easy to Medium
Encounter behaviour

Drace's crew are not expecting anyone. Emlyn Hale is the only person who knows the address and as far as Drace is concerned Emlyn would not dare. The party has genuine surprise available if they approach carefully — particularly via the roof ladder.

Drace makes decisions quickly and does not panic. The moment he assesses the situation as unwinnable he will attempt to negotiate — not surrender, negotiate. He has something the party wants and he knows it. Elbeth is leverage until the moment she is not.

Sot breaks early. Harwick escalates rather than retreats. Brenna reads the situation coldly and acts accordingly. Drace is the variable — everything depends on his assessment of whether the party can be reasoned with.

Elbeth is at the rear of the building. She is injured but coherent and will not need to be carried out. Do not put her in the middle of the fight. If the fight comes to her she will use whatever is at hand — a tool, a barrel stave — but she is not a combatant.

Terrain — Coppergate Stables
The main floorA single storey working space. Tight enough that the party cannot spread out easily. Hay bales on one side create partial cover. The desk and scattered papers give Brenna something to use for flanking.
The balconyAn internal staircase leads to a small raised balcony above the working area. Anyone on the balcony has high ground and line of sight to most of the main floor. Brenna will use it if she can reach it.
The roof ladderExternal, on the outside wall. A party member who climbs it can drop through from above or observe the interior before entering. Drace does not expect anyone to come from the roof.
Elbeth's positionRear sleeping area. Separated from the main working space. Drace will move to stand near her if the fight starts — she is his leverage. Getting to her requires going through or past him.
Drace CR 1/2
Fighter 2 — leader, quick decisions, will negotiate
AC14
HP19
Atk+4
Spd30
Each turn
Shortsword — +4 / (1d6+2) piercing
Hand crossbow — +4 / (1d6+2) piercing range 30/120 ft.
Abilities
Action Surge (1/short rest) — Takes an additional action. Use on the first round if surprised — he recovers fast.
Second Wind (bonus, 1/short rest) — Regains (1d10+2) HP. Use below 10 HP.
Threat Assessment — On his first turn Drace identifies the most dangerous party member and targets them exclusively until they are down or he reassesses.
Tactics
Moves to stand near Elbeth the moment he registers a threat — she is leverage. Targets the most dangerous party member. Will call for negotiation if two of his crew are down or he drops below half HP. His offer: walk away with the girl, no pursuit. He means it. He is pragmatic, not principled.

HP12
Atk+4

Harwick wears a small Broken Wing emblem at his collar. The party may recognise it. He has no connection to the ambush on the road — he is simply a man who believes in something and ended up here. Do not draw attention to the emblem unless the party notices it.
Each turn
Longsword — +4 / (1d8+2) slashing
Handaxe (thrown, round 1) — +4 / (1d6+2) slashing range 20/60 ft.
Abilities
Second Wind (bonus, 1/short rest) — Regains (1d10+1) HP.
Escalates — When an ally drops, Harwick does not retreat. He becomes more committed. His next attack after an ally falls has advantage.
Tactics
Throws handaxe on round one then closes to melee. Positions himself between the party and Drace. Does not yield when others break. The last one standing if it comes to that — not because he is the most capable but because he is the most committed.

Abilities
Breaks early — DC 10 Wis save when first brought below half HP or when any ally is incapacitated. On a fail he drops his weapon and puts his hands up. He is done.

Follows Drace's lead. Swings when told to. Has no investment in the outcome beyond his fee. The moment the situation looks clearly lost he is looking for a way out of it that does not involve getting hurt further.
Brenna CR 1/4
Rogue 1 — cool, tactical, uses the balcony
AC13
HP9
Atk+4
Spd30
Each turn
Shortsword — +4 / (1d6+2) piercing + (1d6) sneak attack if advantage or ally adjacent
Hand crossbow — +4 / (1d6+2) piercing range 30/120 ft. — preferred from balcony
Abilities
Sneak Attack — Once per turn, (1d6) extra damage when she has advantage or an ally is adjacent to the target.
Cunning Action (bonus) — Dash, Disengage, or Hide each turn.
Balcony position — If Brenna reaches the balcony staircase she will go up. From there she has high ground, cover, and line of sight to the whole floor. Use hand crossbow from elevation, sneak attack when an ally is engaged below.
Tactics
Moves immediately toward the balcony staircase on round one using Cunning Action — Dash. Fires from elevation with the crossbow. Descends only if the fight is going badly and she needs to pull someone off Drace. Cold and deliberate. She has done this before.
Elbeth Sable
The objective — injured, angry, walks out under her own power
Elbeth is at the rear of the building. She is the objective. She does not need to be rescued in the traditional sense — she needs the people keeping her here to stop doing that.
Her condition
Injured but coherent — Bruised, dishevelled, two days in a room cataloguing every mistake the people who put her there have made. She walks out under her own power. She is very angry.
In the fight — If the combat reaches her she will use whatever is at hand — a tool from the floor, a barrel stave. She is not a trained combatant but she is not passive either. She has been waiting for this moment.
What she knows — She has been paying attention for two days. She knows Drace's name, Oakstrong's name, and that Emlyn Hale is involved. She will share all of this on the walk back to Seva.
Tactics
Does not fight unless the fight comes to her. Stays at the rear. If Drace moves to use her as leverage she backs away from him and toward whatever exit is available. She is not afraid of him. She is contemptuous of him.
