Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Road from Brindle Hollow
Ambush on the Road
Broken Wing sympathisers attempt to free Marek — Seva Sable witnesses
Combat encounter First combat Level 1 party Moral weight: low — raises later
⚔ Combat Guide
Environment

A road crossing open farmland, running northeast toward Tallowhaven. The party arrives from the west on foot. The road meets a stone bridge crossing a shallow stream to the right. To the left is a large circular ruin — an old stone structure, dry and slightly wrong in character, that nobody uses without quite knowing why. Sheep are scattered across the open ground.

The ambushers were in the area practising — swordwork, archery — when they spotted Marek being escorted. Seva Sable was travelling with one of them, hired the previous evening when he talked too openly about the group's plans. She is present as a witness, not a participant.

The ambushers are positioned on and around the bridge. Brath and Davan charge down when the party enters the road. Idony drops back to the riverbank for cover. Pell holds the bridge with his crossbow.

The bridge Stone, narrow, a natural chokepoint. Pell holds it with a crossbow. High ground and clear sightlines down the road. The party wants to clear this quickly.
The stream Shallow but counts as difficult terrain if crossed. Pell may retreat across it. Idony uses the near bank for cover.
The ruin To the northwest. Circular, dry, slightly unsettling. Players who investigate find nothing explicable. Leave it at that.
The sheep Scattered across the open ground. Anyone running through them causes minor chaos. A small tactical wrinkle and a source of unexpected comedy.
Open ground The party has room to manoeuvre west of the bridge. Ranged characters have clear shots. Cover is limited.
Seva's position Away from the ambushers. Not fighting. Watching with the composed attentiveness of someone making a professional assessment.

Seva Sable Courtesans Network — witness, not combatant
▼
Why she is here

She was hired by one of the ambushers the previous evening after he talked too openly about the group's plans at an inn. She saw an opportunity to gain leverage and intelligence and talked him into bringing her along. She has no stake in whether Marek goes free.

During the encounter
BehaviourProfessionally calm in a chaotic situation. She does not fight, does not flee, does not hide. She watches with the composed attentiveness of someone making a professional assessment. Players who notice her will notice that something about her composure does not fit the circumstances.
If approachedWarm, unhurried, not defensive. She does not explain her presence unless pressed and even then is economical with the truth. She is deciding what the party is useful for.
If threatenedShe does not panic. She makes clear that harming her would be a significant mistake without explaining why. She is correct.
After the encounter

She will appear at whatever inn the party stays at in Tallowhaven. She chose not to be a loose end. She will find them. The party finding her first at the Amber Cup is also possible if they look for her.

Encounter Beats
ObjectiveThe ambushers want Marek free, not dead. The party wants to deliver Marek, not necessarily to fight. Neither side actually wants this to be lethal. That shared disinterest in killing shapes the encounter's tone. It should feel like a scrape, not a battle.
01 The party crests the rise ▼

The party comes over a rise onto the road and sees the bridge ahead. Pell is on the bridge. Brath and Davan are visible on the near side. Idony is at the riverbank edge. Seva is slightly apart, watching.

There is a moment before anything happens. Whether the party recognises it as an ambush depends on Perception checks. A successful check reveals that the group are not simply travellers — their positioning is deliberate.

Marek, behind the party, observes with mild interest. He does not warn them.

02 Brath and Davan charge ▼

Brath and Davan move down from the bridge position toward the party. Davan throws a handaxe on the first round. Pell fires his crossbow from the bridge. Idony repositions along the riverbank looking for an angle.

Roll initiative. The fight is on.

03 The fight ▼

The ambushers are focused on Marek's escape rather than the party's destruction. Brath uses Tactical Shout to create space. Idony targets whoever is physically closest to Marek. Pell suppresses from the bridge.

Marek stands wherever he was when the fight started and watches with the expression of someone waiting for a cart to pass. He does not help either side.

The sheep cause minor chaos if anyone runs through them. Use your judgement on the mechanical effect — disadvantage on the next attack roll from the distraction is reasonable.

Once two ambushers are down Brath calls retreat. They disengage rather than fight to the death. Idony is most likely to escape. Pell is most likely to simply run.

04 Aftermath ▼

The fight ends. The party may have prisoners. They may have questions. They will almost certainly notice Seva if they have not already.

Seva does not attempt to leave. She was never trying to hide. She waits to see what the party does next.

Marek, if asked what that was about, gives an accurate and unhelpful answer. He did not organise it. He did not ask for it. He assumes they were persuaded by his arguments. He is wrong about this but does not know it.

Outcomes and Consequences
Standard — ambushers repelled, Marek still in custody

The escort continues to Tallowhaven. The party may have prisoners to deal with. Seva will find them at the Amber Cup.

Marek released — party lets him go or he escapes

Marek walks away without particular ceremony. Before he goes he gives the party a name — a contact in the Trademasters' Guild in Tallowhaven. Not gratitude. Just information he thinks they might find useful. The party arrives in Tallowhaven without their prisoner and without Merchant access through the Aldermen's Hall.

Marek killed — unlikely but possible

Seva witnessed it. She will not approach them on the road. She will find them in Tallowhaven and make clear, pleasantly and without threat, that she knows what happened and that she is curious about the kind of people who make that choice. The Courtesan thread opens from an unusual angle.

Prisoners taken

The party may take one or more ambushers captive. The magistrate in Tallowhaven will take brief testimony and process the matter. A fine for affray, brief detention, and no further consequence. The hearing the party is asked to attend for will probably never happen. See the Aldermen's Hall encounter for the magistrate scene.

DM Notes
The ruinDo not explain it. If players investigate they find a dry circular structure that feels slightly wrong — no obvious reason, no obvious history, no obvious danger. It is a Zarethian wellspring, drained and empty. Nobody in the world has the context to understand that. Leave it as a feeling and move on.
Seva's composureHer calm during the fight is the tell. She is the most composed person on the road and she is not fighting. Players who notice will file it away. Players who do not will meet her properly at the Amber Cup. Either way she is watching them.
The Broken Wing connectionThe ambushers show the linked-thumbs gesture at some point during or after the encounter — a handshake between two of them, a gesture across the field. The party may notice it. It means nothing to them yet. It will mean something later.
Marek's indifferenceHe watches the fight with mild interest and does nothing. If the party is clearly losing he does not help. If asked why he did not intervene he will say something precise and slightly maddening. He did not ask to be rescued and sees no reason to assist in his own re-arrest. Both of these things are simultaneously true for him.
