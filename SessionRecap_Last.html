At the Wellspring, Wren's fire burned the creature from the pit to nothing. Whatever it was, it does not like fire. Wren's studies told him something worse: the stuff aching in his hand, gathered thickly enough, might begin to think.

You spent the afternoon making the stables your own, burying what needed burying, and at seven you split. At the Amber Cup, Seva told Danzel and the brothers that Oskar Sternov must not reach tomorrow's final negotiations. A hundred gold to anyone with his blood on their hands, as long as nothing led back to her, and nothing made it look like Alric did it. At the Shallow Tuber, Alric told Wren and An that Seva is a ghost who takes from the city and gives nothing back, and that he wants her six feet down. Spells, wizards and the Grandest Library in Abelon, if they brought him good news by morning.

Back at the stables, Wren refused to have any part of murder over town politics. Danzel would not let a man who threatened his family live. So four of you went to Honeywell, where you overheard Oskar ordering Coll to see Alric dead tonight. Danzel offered to do it himself, walked out with two of Oskar's own enchanted daggers, and left Oskar under a suggestion to drink the night away at the Shallow Tuber. In Oskar's desk you found a note: Disappointed to hear the blockade has fallen. Must meet soon. M D P.

Alric opened the back door to you himself. He drew a sword from his cane, and in two rounds he was on his knees, blinded and bleeding. Tenok's spear went through him. And in the doorway stands Coll, who saw all of it, held fast in Jenok's grip.
