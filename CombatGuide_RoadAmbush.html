Combat Reference — Road Ambush

Broken Wing sympathisers — Level 1 party — Seva witnesses
Encounter behaviour

The ambushers want Marek free, not the party dead. They fight with purpose, not fury. Once two ambushers are incapacitated, Brath calls retreat — all remaining combatants attempt to disengage. Idony is most likely to escape. Pell is most likely to simply run.

Seva does not fight unless directly threatened. She does not flee. She watches.
