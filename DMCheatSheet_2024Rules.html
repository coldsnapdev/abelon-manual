DM Cheat Sheet — D&D 2024

Quick rules reference for Abelon sessions
Actions ▼
ActionWhat it does
AttackMake one or more weapon or unarmed strikes. Can grapple or shove as part of an unarmed strike.
Cast a SpellCast a spell with a casting time of 1 action (now called the Magic action).
DashGain extra movement equal to your speed this turn.
DisengageYour movement does not provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn.
DodgeAttacks against you have disadvantage. You have advantage on Dex saves. Ends if incapacitated or speed drops to 0.
HelpGive an ally advantage on their next ability check or attack roll against a creature within 5 ft of you.
HideMake a Stealth check. Must be heavily obscured or behind total cover to attempt.
InfluenceMake a Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation check to shift a creature's attitude. Replaces old Persuasion rules.
MagicActivate a magic item, cast a spell, or use a feature requiring the Magic action.
ReadyPrepare to react to a trigger. Uses your reaction when the trigger occurs. Concentration required for spells.
SearchMake a Perception or Investigation check to find something.
StudyMake an Arcana, History, Nature, or Religion check to recall information about something.
UtiliseUse a non-magical object (open a door, pull a lever, pick something up). Covers most environmental interactions.
Bonus Actions ▼
You only get a bonus action if a feature, spell, or rule grants one. You can only take one bonus action per turn.
Bonus ActionCondition
Off-hand attackWhen you Attack with a Light weapon, attack with another Light weapon in the other hand. No modifier to damage unless Two-Weapon Fighting style.
Bonus action spellCast a spell with a casting time of 1 bonus action. If you do, the only other spell you can cast this turn is a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action.
Class featuresMany class features use bonus actions — Rage, Cunning Action, Second Wind, Wild Shape, Divine Smite, etc.
Equip/stow weaponFree as part of the Attack action in 2024. No longer costs your object interaction.

Resting ▼
Short Rest — 1 hour

Spend Hit Dice to recover HP (roll die + Con modifier per die spent).

Recover class features that recharge on a short rest.

Long Rest — 8 hours

Recover all HP. Regain spent Hit Dice up to half your total (minimum 1).

Recover all spell slots and features that recharge on a long rest.

Reduce Exhaustion by 1 level.

Interrupted by 1 hour of strenuous activity. If you rested 1 hour before interruption, you get a short rest benefit.

Death and Dying ▼
At 0 HP

Fall unconscious. Start making Death Saving Throws at the start of each turn.

Roll a d20. 10 or higher = success. 9 or lower = failure.

3 successes = stable (unconscious, no longer dying).

3 failures = dead.

A natural 20 = regain 1 HP immediately.

A natural 1 = counts as 2 failures.

Taking damage while at 0 HP = 1 failure. Critical hit = 2 failures.

Stabilising

Use an action to make DC 10 Medicine check on an unconscious creature. Success = stable.

Healing spells or any HP recovery also stabilise immediately.

Knocking out: When you reduce a creature to 0 HP with melee, you can knock it out instead of killing it. It is unconscious and starts a Short Rest — stable at the end.

Massive damage

If damage reduces you to 0 HP and there is damage remaining, you die instantly if the remaining damage equals or exceeds your HP maximum.

Combat — Grappling, Shoving, Unarmed ▼
Unarmed Strike — 2024

When you take the Attack action, replace one attack with an unarmed strike.

Choose one: Deal damage (1 + Str mod bludgeoning), Grapple the target, or Shove the target.

Punch and Grab: If you hit with a damaging unarmed strike, you can also attempt to grapple as part of the same strike. Once per turn.

Grappling — 2024

How: Replace an attack with an unarmed grapple. Target must be no more than one size larger. You need one free hand.

DC: 8 + Str mod + proficiency bonus. Target chooses Str or Dex save.

Grappled condition: Speed 0. Disadvantage on attacks against anyone except the grappler.

Grappler advantage: You have advantage on attacks against a creature you are grappling.

Moving a grappled creature: Move at half speed. Free if creature is your size or smaller (Fast Wrestler).

Escaping: Grappled creature uses its action to make a Str or Dex save against your DC.

Shoving — 2024

How: Replace an attack with an unarmed shove. Same size restriction and free hand requirement as grapple.

DC: Same as grapple. Target chooses Str or Dex save.

Effect: Choose — push 5 ft away, or knock Prone.

Two-Weapon Fighting — 2024

Attack with a Light weapon → use bonus action to attack with another Light weapon in other hand.

No ability modifier to the bonus attack's damage (unless you have the Two-Weapon Fighting style).

Now works with ranged Light weapons too. Not limited to melee.

Movement — Climbing, Jumping, Swimming, Falling ▼
TypeCostNotes
Climbing+1 ft per ft (×2 in difficult terrain)Free with Climb Speed. DC set by surface at DM discretion.
Swimming+1 ft per ft (×2 in difficult terrain)Free with Swim Speed.
Crawling+1 ft per ftOnly movement option while Prone.
Difficult terrain+1 ft per ftStacks with climbing/swimming.
Jumping

Long Jump: Running start — up to Str score in feet. Standing — half that. Costs movement equal to distance jumped.

High Jump: Running start — 3 + Str modifier feet. Standing — half that.

Can extend with Athletics check (DM sets DC). Jump counts against movement.

Falling

1d6 bludgeoning per 10 ft fallen, max 20d6 (200 ft).

Damage dealt at end of the fall. Land Prone.

Falling onto a creature: both take falling damage, target makes DC 15 Dex save or is knocked Prone.

Standing from Prone

Costs half your speed (round down). So with 30 ft speed, costs 15 ft to stand.

Vision and Light ▼
Light levelEffect on vision
Bright lightNormal vision. No penalties.
Dim lightLightly obscured. Disadvantage on Perception checks relying on sight.
DarknessHeavily obscured. Effectively Blinded unless you have Darkvision.
SenseHow it works
DarkvisionTreat Darkness as Dim light and Dim light as Bright light within range. Colours appear as shades of grey in Darkness.
BlindsightSee within range without relying on sight. Works in Darkness, against invisible creatures. Still affected by Blinded condition.
TruesightSee through magical darkness, illusions, shapechangers in true form, ethereal plane. Range specified per creature.
Cover

Half cover — +2 to AC and Dex saves. Low walls, furniture, other creatures.

Three-quarters cover — +5 to AC and Dex saves. Portcullises, arrow slits.

Total cover — cannot be directly targeted. Fully concealed by a barrier.

Unseen attackers and targets

Attacking a creature you cannot see: Disadvantage on the attack roll.

Being attacked by an unseen attacker: Attacker has advantage.

The two cancel out if both apply. Must still guess the target's location.

Concentration ▼
What breaks concentration

Taking damage: Con save, DC = max(10, half damage taken). Fail = lose concentration.

Casting another concentration spell: Instantly ends the current one.

Incapacitated or killed.

DM discretion: Extreme environmental distraction may require DC 10 Con save.

Notes

You can end concentration at any time — no action required.

War Caster feat: advantage on Con saves to maintain concentration.

Resilient (Con): proficiency in Con saves.

Conditions ▼
Blinded Cannot see. Auto-fail sight checks. Attacks against you have advantage. Your attacks have disadvantage.
Charmed Cannot attack or target the charmer with harmful effects. Charmer has advantage on social checks against you.
Deafened Cannot hear. Auto-fail hearing checks.
Exhaustion See Exhaustion section. D20 rolls reduced by 2× level. Speed -5 ft per level. Death at level 6.
Frightened Disadvantage on ability checks and attacks while source of fear is in sight. Cannot willingly move closer to it.
Grappled Speed 0. Disadvantage on attacks against anyone except the grappler. Ends if grappler is incapacitated.
Incapacitated Cannot take actions, bonus actions, or reactions. Cannot concentrate. Cannot speak. Disadvantage on initiative.
Invisible Cannot be seen without special senses. Advantage on attacks. Attacks against you have disadvantage. Advantage on initiative.
Paralyzed Incapacitated. Speed 0. Auto-fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks have advantage. Hits within 5 ft are crits.
Petrified Incapacitated. Weight ×10. Resistance to all damage. Immune to poison and disease. Auto-fail Str and Dex saves.
Poisoned Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
Prone Crawling only. Disadvantage on attacks. Melee attacks against you have advantage. Ranged attacks have disadvantage. Stand costs half speed.
Restrained Speed 0. Attacks against you have advantage. Your attacks have disadvantage. Disadvantage on Dex saves.
Stunned Incapacitated. Speed not affected (changed in 2024). Auto-fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks against you have advantage.
Unconscious Incapacitated. Speed 0. Prone. Auto-fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks have advantage. Hits within 5 ft are crits.
Exhaustion — 2024 ▼
Level 1-2 to all d20 rolls. Speed -5 ft.
Level 2-4 to all d20 rolls. Speed -10 ft.
Level 3-6 to all d20 rolls. Speed -15 ft.
Level 4-8 to all d20 rolls. Speed -20 ft.
Level 5-10 to all d20 rolls. Speed -25 ft.
Level 6Death.
Each Long Rest removes 1 level. Greater Restoration removes 1 level. Does not reset on death — revived creatures keep exhaustion minus 1.
