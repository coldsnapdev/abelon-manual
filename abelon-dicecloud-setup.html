A Guide to Setting Up Your Character in DiceCloud

Before the adventure begins in Tallowhaven, your character needs a home. DiceCloud is the character sheet platform we use for this campaign. Follow these steps carefully and you'll be ready for Session Zero.

1

Create Your Account

Head to dicecloud.com and create a free account. Once logged in, you're ready to set up your character sheet.

2

Create a New Character

Navigate to the Characters tab in the sidebar and click the large red + button in the bottom-right corner. Fill in your character's name, gender, and other basic details, then click Create.

Don't stress about locking anything in at this stage — all of this can be changed later.

3

Read (or Skip) the Tutorial

After creating your character, DiceCloud will offer you an in-app tutorial. It's worth reading if this is your first time in v2 — it explains the slot-based build system well. When you're done (or ready to move on), click the delete icon next to the "Introduction to DiceCloud V2" card to dismiss it.

Note: You can always revisit the tutorial later via the Sources panel.

4

Subscribe to the Right Libraries

This is the most important step. DiceCloud uses libraries — community-built collections of rules, classes, races, spells, and items — to populate your character sheet. Without the right ones, your Build tab will be almost empty.

On your character's Build page, click the large red Sources box at the top. Click Browse Community Libraries. You'll see a list of available libraries — first, unsubscribe from the DiceCloud 5th Edition SRD (the default), then find and subscribe to Tameran's Library of Everything (2024).

Why Tameran's? It's the most complete and actively maintained 5e (2024) library, covering classes, species, backgrounds, spells, and items through a suite of interlinked sub-libraries — all set up to work together out of the box.

Once you've subscribed, return to the Sources panel and click Insert. On the next screen, select the following bundles:

  • # Bundle: Core Sourcebooks — PHB, DMG, MM
  • # Bundle: Core Supplements
  • # Bundle: Setting Supplements (2024)
  • # Bundle: Additional Character Options (2024)

Click Insert at the bottom when done, then close the Sources panel by clicking the red X.

5

Fill Your Character's Slots

With your libraries loaded, your Build page will now show a series of red boxes — these are slots, waiting to be filled. Think of each one as a blank on your character sheet that DiceCloud wants you to complete. Click the + button on each slot to make your selection.

Work through the following in order:

  • Species — select your species
  • Background — select your background
  • Starting Class — select your class and subclass when prompted
  • Spell Slot Structure Spellcasters only — select Jon's Spell Slot Structure (it should be the only option shown)
  • [JSSS] Spellcasting Type Spellcasters only — select Spellcasting

Tip: Most slots are self-explanatory. Work through them top to bottom and click all the red boxes until they disappear. If you're unsure about a choice, make your best guess — we'll go through characters together at Session Zero.

6

Set Your Extensions

There will be an Extensions slot on your Build page. Click the + and select all four of the following:

  • Dice Roller
  • Show Temp HP
  • Automatic Hit Dice
  • Inspiration Checkbox

You can close the Extensions slot once those three are selected.

7

Set Base Ability Score Method to Manual

Find the Base Ability Score slot and set it to Manual. Leave the actual scores blank for now — we'll be rolling stats together during Session Zero, so nothing to fill in here yet.

At this point, your Build page should show exactly three remaining red boxes: Base Ability Scores, Extensions, and Multiclass. That's correct — you're done with the Build tab.

8

This links your DiceCloud rolls to our Discord server so everyone can see them during play. Here's how:

  • From your character sheet, click the three-dot menu (top right)
  • Select Edit Details
  • Scroll down and open the Settings section
  • Find the Discord Webhook URL field and paste in the following:

What this does: Any dice rolls made from your character sheet will automatically post to the campaign's Discord channel, keeping everyone's rolls visible and accountable at the table.

9

Fill In What You Know

Head to the Journal tab on your character sheet. Feel free to write in as much or as little about your character as you like — backstory, personality notes, appearance, motivations. Nothing here is final. Think of it as a sketchpad for your character concept ahead of Session Zero.

The Stats, Features, Inventory, and Spells tabs will populate automatically based on your slot selections. Take a look around — it should already feel like a real character sheet.
