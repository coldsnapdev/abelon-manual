Combat Reference — Assassination Paths

Three private rooms — two combatants each — Level 3 party — fights to the end

Encounter 1 — Seva Sable and Mira / Private Room, The Amber Cup

Encounter behaviour

A small room. The table limits movement. Mira is at the door — between the party and the exit. Seva is seated when the party enters. She reads the room in two seconds and does not show it. Neither combatant flees. Neither yields. Seva remains calm throughout — the quieter she gets, the more dangerous she is.

The fight is contained. The Amber Cup is busy below. No one will hear it until it is over.

Encounter 2 — Alric Venn and Huw / Private Room, The Shallow Tuber

Encounter behaviour

A private dining room. The table runs the length of the space. Huw is at the door. Alric is seated. He has never been in a fight. He commits with desperate energy — frightened underneath the fury and will not show it. Huw is far more capable than anyone realised. Neither flees. Neither yields.

The Shallow Tuber is quieter than the Amber Cup. Other diners are present. The fight needs to end before someone opens the door.

Alric Venn
CR 1 — desperate energy — has never done this before
AC12
HP22
Atk+4
Spd30
Each turn
Rapier — +4 / 1d8+2 piercing
Abilities
Suggestion (1 slot) — DC 13 Wis save — one target follows a reasonable suggestion for 8 hours. Last resort only — cornered, below 5 HP.

Fights with feeling rather than skill. Angrier than he has ever been — not at the party exactly, at the failure of his own systems. That anger makes him less predictable than usual. Uses Suggestion only if cornered and out of options. Does not flee. Stands his ground. Does not give them the satisfaction of running.

Draws immediately. Cold in a way that surprises people who have only seen him taking notes. Charm Person on round 1 — most dangerous party member. Positions to protect Alric rather than engage independently. Fights until he cannot. Does not break.

Encounter 3 — Oskar Sternov and Tav / Honeywell Office, After Hours

Encounter behaviour

A cramped office above a banked forge. The desk dominates. One exit — the stairs — where Tav is positioned. The window onto the forge floor below is glass and a fifteen-foot drop. Nobody is fleeing that way. Oskar says nothing during the fight. Not a word. The silence is the most unsettling thing about fighting him in a small room at night.

Tav throws on round 1 then closes. Neither combatant flees. Neither yields.
