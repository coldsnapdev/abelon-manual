Read-aloud recap

Over breakfast at the Amber Cup, Seva asked Danzel to bring his friends to meet her there at seven tonight, and Alric asked you to come to the Shallow Tuber, also at seven. With hours to spare, you rode north to the dry Aeronic wellspring to test the ring and the box.

The ring did nothing. The box buzzed faintly, the way it had at Everbloom, which is odd at a place that should belong to Aeron. Then Costin walked up from under the bridge. He told you relics of the gods need a power source, and that an hour of quiet contemplation near one lets a relic speak to you and become yours. He was surprised a source exists here at all: no sign of Aeron, and stranger still, his brother is here. He wants nothing from the ledger. He was a young hand at the shrine when Adelina Thorncroft was shrinekeeper, a woman with secrets who loved the catacombs, and she once told him to find her ledger if he ever grew as curious as she was. The shrine is usually deserted after six. His one request: don't take from the shrine without replacing what you take.

Wren sat on the lip of the pit and spent the hour with the box; Tenok spent it with Dohyuk's ring, which stayed silent. The box is Wren's now. It unmakes whatever is shut inside it. Testing it, Wren dropped a gem in and drew it out blackened, and felt his hand begin to die: a Rot infestation, weakening and slowing him.

Then something climbed out of the pit. Animal and not animal, it slithers and walks, made of the same black stuff that came out of RJ and coursed through the wolves. An put a dagger in it, then another. Danzel's mockery found no mind to land on. And that's where we stopped.
