Day 2: Edran's Waystone

29 Saeleth. A worn relief map at a fork in the road, a courier resting beside it, and something seeping into camp that night.
At a glanceA waystone of Edran (minor god of the Map) at a fork. Travellers trace their route on it for luck. Someone has recently scratched a hurried line east. A wary westbound courier is resting there. Night 2 is the first Rot on the road.

The waystone

  • A worn relief map carved into the stone. Travellers trace the route they mean to take with a finger.
  • Someone has recently scratched a hurried line heading east.
  • The Elvish inscription explains the custom:
"Trace the way you mean to go, and the Mapmaker will remember you upon it."
