Mission Overview

Mira finds a party member and delivers Seva's message. The private table upstairs at the Amber Cup. Seva explains that Elbeth Sable has been missing for two days. She received a threatening note — something about a starling, clearly meant as a warning — from a member of the Driftwood Point delegation named Emlyn Hale. She believes Elbeth is being held. She needs the party to find her and get her out safely before the deadline.

Seva wants Elbeth back. She does not want the delegation touched. She does not want a scene during festival week. She pays half upfront.

What Seva does not know yet is that Emlyn is not in control of the situation. He is as trapped as Elbeth is, in his own way.

The hidden consequence The downstream effect on the Merchants is entirely opaque to the party. Emlyn Hale returns to his delegation duties shaken and noncommittal about a clause Alric was counting on. Alric notices. He looks at the Courtesans. The party never sees this connection.
The easter egg Harwick is wearing something with a Broken Wing emblem — a patch, a pin, cloth tied at the wrist. He is not an operative. He just found the symbol and the feeling resonated. A party that notices has now seen it in three contexts. It means nothing yet.

Who he is

A young man of good family whose respectable position in the Driftwood Point delegation does not quite match his private habits. Posh, well connected, and catastrophically out of his depth. He sent the threatening note before the kidnapping happened — he wanted to sound powerful. The thugs were supposed to make a vague point. Instead they took Elbeth, realised how much leverage that gave them, and started extorting him. He is now being extorted by everyone involved and cannot tell anyone without explaining why.

Finding him

He is in his room at the Anchor and Reed, the inn where the Driftwood delegation is staying. He has barely left it in two days. He is not hiding — he has nowhere to go. A request for Emlyn Hale at the front desk is all it takes to find him.

Getting him to talk
First approachHe stonewalls. He is frightened of Seva, frightened of the thugs, frightened of the delegation finding out. DC 13 Persuasion or Intimidation to break through. He is not good at holding the line.
Once talkingHe tells them everything. The note, the muscle, the intermediary, the fact that it all went wrong. He does not have Elbeth's location because he hired through an intermediary and the intermediary handles everything. He gives them Oakstrong's name and where to find him.
His mannerFrightened, slightly pathetic, genuinely relieved that someone is trying to fix it. He may cry. He is not proud of any of this. The party will almost feel sorry for him. Almost.
What he cannot give them
Elbeth's locationHe genuinely does not know it. Oakstrong handles that. This is not evasion.

Who he is

A fixer operating in Tallowhaven's working quarter who chose the name Oakstrong himself, probably relatively recently, and says it with complete sincerity. He connects supply and demand and takes a fee. He is not a villain. He is a professional who facilitated a job that has since escalated far beyond what he agreed to, and he has his own reasons for wanting it resolved quietly.

Finding him

Emlyn gives the party his name and directs them to the Tallow and Grain, a working quarter establishment where Oakstrong tends to conduct business. He is not hiding. A DC 12 Investigation or streetwise approach locates him without much difficulty.

Getting the address
His positionHe will not give up a client's location easily. Professional reputation. He says this with complete sincerity and the name Oakstrong. DC 14 Persuasion or a reasonable bribe gets it. Making the alternative sufficiently unappealing also works. He is pragmatic.
Why he cooperatesDrace has escalated beyond what Oakstrong agreed to facilitate. A hostage situation is considerably more serious than the original instruction. He has his own exposure here and a professional interest in this resolving quietly. He gives the address because it is the sensible thing to do.
His mannerAttempts to project dangerous competence. Mostly succeeds because people do not look closely enough to notice the gap between the image and the reality. He says Oakstrong like it is the most natural name in the world. The party should find this faintly amusing without it undercutting the scene.
Investigation Beats
I.1 Seva's brief — the private table ▼

Mira has already done her work. The party is upstairs. Seva is already seated. She gets to it more quickly than usual — she does not have the same patience for preamble when someone she cares about is missing.

She describes the situation, the note, Emlyn Hale's name, the inn. She pays half upfront. She says she will find them when it is done. Her manner makes clear that done means Elbeth is safe, not merely located.

I.2 Finding Emlyn ▼

The Anchor and Reed is not difficult to find — it is the respectable inn where the Driftwood delegation has been quartered for festival week. A request for Emlyn Hale at the front desk. He is in his room. He has been in his room for two days.

He opens the door looking terrible. He was not expecting visitors and is visibly calculating whether these particular visitors are good or bad news. He tries to stonewall. He is not good at it. Once the party applies any meaningful pressure he starts talking and does not stop easily.

He tells them everything. He gives them Oakstrong's name and the Tallow and Grain. He apologises several times. He may cry at some point. He genuinely does not know where Elbeth is.

I.3 Finding Oakstrong ▼

The Tallow and Grain is a working quarter establishment — busy enough that a conversation does not stand out, plain enough that nobody asks questions. Oakstrong is there conducting business with the unruffled air of someone who considers himself a professional. He introduces himself as Oakstrong without any indication that this requires explanation.

He resists giving the address on professional grounds. He does not resist for long. Drace's escalation has left him exposed and he knows it. A bribe, a persuasive argument, or a clear statement of consequences gets the address. He gives it with the dignity of someone making a pragmatic decision rather than capitulating.

He also mentions, as the party leaves, that whatever happens in there is nothing to do with him. He says this to their backs. He means it.

Phase Two — The Coppergate Stables
The Location

The Coppergate Stables — a single storey working stables on a quiet street in the working quarter. Drace has access to the main building, which serves as both workspace and holding space. The stable yard outside has hay bales stacked along the fence line, a horse pen with a trough, and enough coming and going of animals and workers during the day that the building does not stand out.

The main building has two points of entry beyond the front: an internal staircase leading up to a small mezzanine balcony, and an external ladder on the outside of the building going up to the roof. A careful party can use the roof ladder to get above the building and observe or drop in from an unexpected angle. The balcony overlooks the main working area below.

Elbeth is being held in the sleeping area at the rear of the ground floor. She is injured — the initial confrontation was not gentle — but she is coherent and very angry. She has been paying attention to everything she can observe from where she is sitting for two days. She walks out under her own power.

Drace and the others are not expecting a rescue party. Emlyn does not know the address, so as far as Drace is concerned the only person who could lead someone here is Oakstrong, and Oakstrong is professional. The element of surprise is available to a party that approaches carefully — particularly if they use the roof ladder.

Elbeth Sable
Load
portrait
Elbeth Sable The one being rescued — injured, furious, has been paying attention
▼
When the party finds her

She is in a back room. Bruised face, injured but coherent. She is not frightened. She is angry in the specific way of someone who has had two days to sit with a situation and has used every minute of it productively. She has paid attention to everything she could observe — sounds, voices, routines, the layout of the space beyond her door as much as she could deduce it.

Her manner
Walking outShe walks out under her own power. She makes a point of this. Do not have the party carry her or support her unless she specifically requests it, which she will not.
On the way backShe says very little. She is saving it for Seva. The party should feel the weight of what happened without it being narrated at them.
On EmlynIf the party mentions him she makes a sound that is not quite a laugh. Zero sympathy. She knew exactly what kind of person he was before any of this happened. That is, in a sense, how this started.
What she knows
Information gatheredShe has been listening for two days. She has a detailed picture of Drace's operation, his habits, his relationship with the others. She passes this to Seva. It is not relevant to the party's immediate mission but Seva will find it useful.
Combat Beats
Tight quartersThis fight happens in a small space. The party cannot spread out, flanking is harder to establish, and anyone who goes down is immediately in a dangerous position. Drace knows the room. The party does not. Use that asymmetry in the first round.
C.1 The approach ▼

The party has the address. They approach the location. A careful approach — Stealth DC 13 — allows them to reach the door without being heard. A successful check means they choose when the fight starts. A failed check means Drace has a round of preparation.

There is no back exit. This is not a bridge with two ends. It is a room with a door and the party is standing at it.

C.2 The fight ▼

Four against the party in close quarters. Drace directs the others efficiently. Brenna is the most dangerous and does not break easily. Sot and Harwick follow Drace's lead.

The Broken Wing emblem on Harwick is visible during the fight if a player looks — a patch, a pin, something worn rather than displayed. Do not draw attention to it. Let it be found.

Drace will offer to walk away if the fight turns clearly against him. He has no ideological commitment. He makes the offer practically, not desperately. If the party accepts he takes Sot and Harwick and leaves. Brenna makes her own decision.

C.3 Finding Elbeth ▼

The back room. She is there. She looks at whoever opens the door with the expression of someone who has been waiting and is not going to make it easy by being grateful.

She stands up. She walks to the door. She goes with the party.

Outcomes
Elbeth recovered, thugs dealt with

The party returns to the Amber Cup. Seva receives Elbeth in private. When she comes back out she pays the second half and asks her one question about Emlyn. She files the answer. Something will happen to Emlyn Hale after the festival. It will not be loud. The party is not told what it is.

Elbeth recovered, Drace walked away

Seva pays the second half. She notes that Drace is still out there. She does not say this as a criticism. She is simply noting it. She will deal with it. She always does.

Deadline missed but Elbeth recovered

Seva pays the second half. The deadline passing had consequences she does not share with the party. She is grateful Elbeth is safe. The rest is her problem to manage.

Elbeth not recovered

Seva does not get angry at the party. She becomes very still. She thanks them for trying. She will handle it herself. The Courtesan relationship does not close but it is different now. She trusted them with something that mattered and it did not work. That is information she will carry.

DM Notes
Emlyn Hale's fateSeva will deal with him after the festival. Not physically. She is not that crude. But the leverage the network held over him is now accompanied by the knowledge of what he did. He will spend a long time being very careful and very cooperative. He will also become notably noncommittal about a specific clause in the Driftwood deal. Alric will notice. The party will not connect these things. That is correct.
Elbeth walking outDo not soften this. She is injured and she walks out under her own power. It is a statement about who she is and what the network is. She has been through something difficult and she is not going to perform vulnerability for the party's benefit. Let it be what it is.
The Broken Wing emblemDo not mention it in narration unless a player specifically looks at Harwick. If they do notice and ask about it, Harwick does not explain it — it is personal, not something he discusses with people trying to hit him. The party carries the observation. It joins the pattern that will mean something in Act 2.
OakstrongPlay him with complete sincerity. He chose the name. He stands by it. The comedy is in how seriously he takes himself, not in the party laughing at him. He is useful and practical and his name is Oakstrong. These things coexist.
