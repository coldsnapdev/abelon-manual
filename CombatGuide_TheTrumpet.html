Combat Reference — The Trumpet

Three waves — escalating — the room empties — Level 3 party — Medium
Encounter behaviour

This is a chaotic pub fight, not a tactical engagement. The combatants arrive in three waves and the room empties around them as uninvolved patrons flee. The first wave is impaired and manageable. The second wave is more serious. The third wave — specifically Davan Holt — is the real threat and the party will not see him coming until round three.

Wave 1 — Perran's table: Perran, Aldwyn, Osric, Fenn. Fenn runs in round one. The rest are drunk and committed in varying degrees.

Wave 2 — the bar: Brynn and Mags. Arrive when the table fight draws their attention. Brynn is a brawler. Mags has already decided what she is going to do.

Wave 3 — the door and back: Davan Holt at the door, Jori out the back. Davan has been watching all evening. He is the most dangerous person in the room and the party will not realise this until he moves. Jori is deeply reluctant.

The room thins out fast. By the end of round one the uninvolved patrons have fled. The party has more space than they started with. That space will feel different when Davan Holt walks through the door in round three.
