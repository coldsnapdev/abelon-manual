Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Brindle Hollow
The Stillwater Shrine
Collecting Marek Talan — introduction to the VTT environment
Social / exploration No combat expected Tutorial scene First encounter with Marek
Environment

The Stillwater Shrine is not a building. It is an open space at the centre of a cluster of buildings that belongs to the Aeronist community in Brindle Hollow. At its heart is a circular stone pool — the shrine itself. The water is still and clear. Offerings have been left at the edge: flowers, small tools, a piece of cloth. The pool is dedicated to Aeron, whose domain includes growth and the body's vitality. Water as the source of all growth.

The ceremony has been and gone. The space has the particular quiet of somewhere that was recently busy and is no longer. Evidence of the disruption is present but minor — the ceremony was not violent, just ruined.

Marek is near the cage at the south of the map. He is sitting or standing quietly. He does not look distressed.

The pool The shrine itself. Still, clear, with a stone surround. Offerings at the edge. A natural focal point for investigation. Religion and history checks can reveal information about Aeron's domain.
The cage A simple holding cage near the south entrance. Marek is being held here. It is not comfortable but it is not cruel either.
Ceremony remnants The preparation for the seeding is visible. The animal was not sacrificed — Marek's disruption prevented it. Players who look carefully can piece together what happened.
Surrounding buildings The shrine complex. Morfudd's residence is here. The space is communal rather than monumental — a working religious community, not a grand institution.
The offerings Small, practical things. Tools, food, cloth. Not precious objects. Worship as habit rather than performance. This is what Aeronist faith looks like at the village level.
Atmosphere The space feels slightly diminished. The ceremony did not happen. Something that was supposed to occur did not. Players may feel this without being able to name it.

Marek Talan Former Paladin of Aeron — the prisoner
▼
Appearance

Late forties, close-cropped grey hair, strong jaw, weathered face with deep-set eyes that are calm rather than warm. Plain functional clothing. No holy symbols. No armour. The bearing of someone who was once devoted to something and has not replaced it with anything else. Both hands visible — the ring fingers are missing from each hand to the second knuckle.

The missing fingers

Do not draw attention to them. Let players notice. If asked, Marek answers briefly and without drama — a temple accident, years ago. He does not elaborate. He does not perform emotion about it. It is simply a fact about his hands.

Personality
Default modePatient detachment. He is not troubled by his arrest. He is waiting to see what happens next, as he waits to see what happens with most things.
On the ceremonyHe will explain his objection if asked. The animal taken was not replaced. The world is down something and gets nothing in return. He says this plainly, without heat, as if it is obvious.
On his faithHe does not perform disillusionment. He simply does not speak about Aeron as something he currently belongs to. Questions about his faith are answered briefly and accurately and then he moves on.
On the partyHe assesses them quietly. He is not impressed by competence and not dismissive of it. He will talk with players who engage him seriously and disengage from players who perform at him.
On being escortedHe does not resist. He does not thank anyone for anything. He goes where he is taken with the mild patience of someone who has somewhere else to be eventually.
What he will say if asked directly
"The seeding takes something. Nothing returns. The world is smaller for it. I said so. I was asked to leave. I said it again. Here we are."

Encounter Beats
Purpose of this sceneThis is the tutorial encounter. The party gets comfortable with the VTT, moves their tokens, talks to NPCs, and investigates a space before anything consequential happens. There is no combat. The only objective is to collect Marek and leave. Everything else is optional enrichment.
01 Arrival at the shrine ▼

The party arrives at the Stillwater Shrine. The pool is the first thing they see — still, clear, with offerings at the edge. Marek is visible near the cage at the south. Dav is nearby. The aftermath of the ceremony is present but not dramatic.

Fen is tidying. Idris is sitting somewhere comfortable. Casimir is near the pool. Brin is somewhere between disappointed and angry. Maren wants to leave.

02 Free exploration ▼

Let the party investigate the space and talk to people before they collect Marek. This is the scene's purpose — getting comfortable with the environment and the VTT tools.

The pool rewards investigation. A Religion check reveals information about Aeron's domain and the significance of the seeding ceremony. A History check places the shrine in the context of Brindle Hollow's community. A Perception check notices the ceremony preparations — the animal that was never used, the offerings arranged in a specific pattern.

All NPCs are available for conversation. None of them are urgent.

03 Meeting Marek ▼

When the party approaches Marek he receives them with the calm patience of someone who has been waiting and is not particularly bothered by how long it took.

He will answer questions. He does not perform distress about his situation. If asked about the ceremony he explains his objection simply and without heat. If asked about his hands he answers briefly and moves on. If asked about his faith he is accurate and brief.

He is not grateful. He is not hostile. He is simply present and ready to go when they are.

04 Taking custody ▼

Dav hands over custody without ceremony. He is relieved to be done with it. Maren's expression makes her feelings clear. Dav thanks the party with the practical warmth of someone who has completed an obligation and can now go home.

Marek submits to whatever arrangement the party makes for the escort — bound, unbound, walking alongside, whatever. He has no preference. He goes where he is taken.

The party leaves the shrine. The road to Tallowhaven begins.

DM Notes
The missing fingersDo not mention them in your description of Marek unless a player specifically looks at his hands. If they do notice and ask, Marek's answer is brief. The symbol his followers make with their hands is an imitation of this injury. The party will not know that yet. Plant it quietly and let it surface later.
Marek's oathHe is still technically a Paladin of Aeron. His oath is intact. His faith is the wound, not his vow. He does not identify himself as a paladin but he will not deny it if asked directly.
The shrine as seedThe pool is a shrine to Aeron. Aeron's absence is one of the campaign's hidden truths. Players who investigate carefully may feel that something about the space is slightly thin — worship without a clear recipient. Do not confirm this. Just let the feeling exist.
No rushThis scene has no clock. There is no reason for the party to hurry. If they spend an hour talking to Idris or playing cards with no one in particular, that is the scene working correctly. The road comes next.
