Finding Marek

What the people of Tallowhaven can tell the party about where Marek Talan has gone.
Where he isHollin's Stage, an old ferry landing at the far east end of the Docks, unused since the new quay was built. Marek spends his mornings replacing its rotten planks one at a time, never pulling up an old board until he has its replacement in hand. He sleeps in the old fare hut. He doesn't fight back if attacked, dockhands pass by and know him as the harmless man fixing the stage, and the old grey half of the jetty is rotten enough to give way under a scuffle.
Using this pageInformation isn't tied to any particular person: give each piece to whoever the party talks to. The DC is one number covering both how likely someone is to know it and how willing they'd be to share it. Choose the check that fits how the party asks. On a failure, or if the approach was poor, use a line from the vague answers at the bottom instead.

Useful information

Roughly in order: the higher the DC, the closer it brings the party to Hollin's Stage.

12The last time he spoke to anyone, he said he'd rather do than talk.
12He never painted the marks himself, and he didn't seem to like them.

13He's about in the mornings. By midday he's gone again.
14A big, quiet man buys offcut planks and a pot of pitch from a chandler on the Docks most mornings, and pays without haggling.
15He carries his timber east along the waterfront, away from the new quay.
17"You mean the fellow fixing Hollin's Stage? The old ferry landing? Nobody's used that in ten years."
19He sleeps in the old fare hut at the landward end of the stage.

Red herrings

Things people say with confidence that won't lead to Marek.

DCWhat they hearThe truth
10He's left the city, gone back to Crossroads.False. His followers believe it.
11He's been drinking at the Copper Vine most evenings.False. Idle gossip about any stranger with a following.
12He's sick, laid up at Serastra's Rest in the Furrows.False. The Mercy House hasn't seen him, though the party may find Huw there.
13He's preaching out by the Stillwater road now.A copycat using his words, bent harsher. Anyone who heard Marek can tell it isn't him.
14Follow the painted marks: they get thicker towards a cellar in the Tallow where his people meet.True, but it leads to his followers, who don't know where he is and don't welcome outsiders. The scene most likely to turn violent.

Vague answers

For failed checks or poor roleplay. None of them is false, and several nudge gently in the right direction.

  • "Marek? The preacher? Haven't heard him in a while. Thought he'd moved on."
  • "Ask the performers in Cooper's Square. They know everyone who's ever stood on that step."
  • "Them with the painted crosses? Ask them, not me."
  • "Quiet sort, for a man who used to shout from a box."
  • "Last I saw him he was heading towards the water. Or the market. One of the two."
  • "Folk like that never stay anywhere long."
  • "Try the mornings. Everybody's somewhere in the mornings."
  • "You're not the first to ask after him this week." (They can't say who else did.)
  • "Smelled like a shipwright, last time he walked past. Odd, for a preacher."
  • "I don't want trouble with his lot. Ask someone else."
