Combat Guide — The Festival Finale

Cooper's Square — three faction paths — Level 3 party — Hard to Deadly
Encounter behaviour — all paths

The faction arrives in waves. The first wave is the most numerous but least capable — they create chaos and buy time for the real threats to commit. The faction leader hangs back until the crew takes significant losses, then commits with anger overcoming caution. If the leader is the last one standing they attempt to flee.

The fight is personal. These are not strangers. Whether the party pulls its blows, what they do when the leader tries to run, and how they treat the people who break — all of it matters and all of it has consequences in Act 2.

Alric's faction — Merchants

Huw leads. The polite assistant who booked their table is composed and cold in a way the party did not anticipate. Weapons skew toward quality purchased steel — longswords, rapiers, light crossbows. Huw has Charm Person. The merchants grabbed what was available.

Eight combatants total. Prys and Nesta break in round one. Bael and Gethin carry through wave one. Huw and Aldric are wave two. Alric arrives when Huw is in serious trouble.

Cors Holdworn leads. He was called before the meeting started — that level of preparation tells the party something about Oskar they did not fully appreciate. Weapons are craft tools and working weapons — warhammers, hand axes, mallets. Ysolde has Thunderwave. Nobody expects a master weaver to cast anything.

Seven combatants total. Mael breaks early. Tav and Efa Dun carry through wave one. Drest, Cors, and Ysolde are wave two — Ysolde casts once and leaves. Oskar arrives when Cors is struggling.

Seva's faction — Courtesans

Elbeth arrives first. The party last saw her walking out of Coppergate Stables under her own power. She has had time to think about this moment. The Sables are trained and composed — the most dangerous wave one combatants across all three paths. Cors steps sideways from the Trademaster group during wave two. Seva is the most dangerous thing the party faces tonight.

Eight combatants total. The associates create chaos. The operatives and Mira apply real pressure. Cors's arrival is a surprise. Seva is the finale.

Seva's Deflect reactionWhen Seva is hit by a melee attack she can see, she reduces the damage by (1d6+3). This is her existing stat block ability. Use it — it is the moment the party realises they are in a different kind of fight.
The escape attemptEach faction leader — Alric, Oskar, Seva — attempts to flee if last standing. Whether the party lets them go is a genuine moral choice with Act 2 consequences. Do not resolve it for them.
Cors Holdworn across pathsIn the teal path Cors fights for Oskar as wave 2. In the pink path he steps sideways to the Sables also in wave 2. In the blue path he is not present — he left with Anneth. His secret loyalty is a background thread. Only pull it if a player tugs at it.
Anneth HoldwornShe does not fight in any path. She may be present in Cooper's Square watching. She may have already left. Let the session decide. If she is present she watches everything with the quiet satisfaction of someone who has been patient for a very long time.
