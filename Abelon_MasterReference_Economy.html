Good standing +5 gp Clean delivery, good relationship, treated the employer with respect
Standard standing +0 gp Job done, no complications, professional
Poor standing −5 gp Partial delivery, friction, or handled it in a way the employer disliked
Upfront payments are staticThe upfront is a commitment — paid because that is how the employer operates, not because they trust the party yet. The completion payment is where the relationship registers.

Magic item pool — Act 1
Distribution philosophyThese items are a pool to draw from — not assigned to specific locations. Deploy them when the moment calls for it: a high investigation roll, a dramatically appropriate beat, a player who has been unlucky. You are exercising authorial judgement about when the world rewards them, not lying about where things are.

Isolde's Immovable Rod
TextureUncommon
▼
Looks likeA plain iron rod about two feet long with a small button at one end. Unremarkable. Slightly heavier than expected.
MechanicsPress the button and the rod locks in space — immovable regardless of gravity, up to 8,000 lbs of force. Press again to release. No attunement required. Unlimited uses.
In AbelonOne of Isolde's objects — divine rather than magical. The rod locks its own form in space absolutely, refusing to allow any force to change the position it occupies. The button is the mechanism by which it decides whether its form is fixed or fluid.
Tell a playerPlayers will figure out the rest. Do not over-explain it.
Isolde's Shifting Pigments
TextureUncommon
▼
Looks likeA small wooden box containing several pots of pigment in different colours. The pigments smell faintly of something that is not quite paint. A worn brush is included.
MechanicsWhatever is painted becomes real — physically present, correct texture and weight, fully functional. Limit: anything worth more than 25 gp will look right but use inferior materials. No attunement required. Houseruled.
HouseruleThe paint creates objects on surfaces, not the space those objects imply. A painted door is a real door flush with the wall behind it — open it and there is stone. A painted pit is a real depression an inch deep. Objects created are real and persist. A painted bridge painted onto a cliff face is a real bridge attached to a cliff face.
In AbelonOne of Isolde's objects. A craftsman's tool with an inexplicable property — medieval 3D printer, not high-magic oddity.
