Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Brindle Hollow
The Stillwater Shrine
Collecting Marek Talan — introduction to the VTT environment
Social / explorationNo combat expectedTutorial sceneFirst encounter with Marek

The party arrives at an Aeronist shrine to collect Marek Talan, the former paladin under citizen's arrest. There's no clock and no danger — this is a tutorial scene for getting comfortable with the VTT, so the party explores the shrine, talks to whoever's around, meets Marek, and takes custody of him for the road to Tallowhaven.

At a glance
Purpose: tutorial scene — no combat, no clock. The only real objective is to collect Marek; everything else is optional enrichment.
Marek: calm, patient, not distressed. Missing both ring fingers to the second knuckle — don't mention it unless a player looks.
His view on the ceremony: the seeding takes something and nothing replaces it — he said so, was asked to leave, said it again
Taking custody: Dav hands Marek over without ceremony, relieved to be done with it. Marek has no preference how he's escorted.
Location Descriptions
The Stillwater Shrine

Not a building — an open space at the centre of a cluster of buildings belonging to Brindle Hollow's Aeronist community. At its heart, a circular stone pool, still and clear, with offerings left at the edge: flowers, small tools, a piece of cloth. The ceremony has been and gone; the space has the particular quiet of somewhere that was recently busy and no longer is. A simple holding cage stands near the south entrance.

NPCs
Marek Talan Former Paladin of Aeron — the prisoner
▼
Visual description

Late forties, close-cropped grey hair, strong jaw, weathered face, calm rather than warm eyes. Plain functional clothing, no holy symbols, no armour. Both ring fingers missing to the second knuckle on each hand — don't draw attention to it, let players notice. If asked, he answers briefly: a temple accident, years ago. No elaboration.

Goals for this encounter

None beyond patience — he doesn't resist, doesn't thank anyone, goes where he's taken.

What he knows

Explains his objection to the ceremony plainly if asked: the animal taken was not replaced, the world is down something and gets nothing in return. States it as obvious, without heat.

What he keeps secret

Doesn't perform disillusionment about his faith — simply doesn't speak of Aeron as something he currently belongs to. Answers questions about it briefly and accurately, then moves on.

Personality points

Patient detachment — not troubled by his arrest, waiting to see what happens next as he waits with most things. Assesses the party quietly; not impressed by competence, not dismissive of it. Engages players who are serious, disengages from players who perform at him. If asked directly: “The seeding takes something. Nothing returns. The world is smaller for it. I said so. I was asked to leave. I said it again. Here we are.”

Combat

Paladin 3, Oath of the Ancients — faith intact, god absent. AC 14, HP 25. Won't initiate; if compelled by magic, fights efficiently and without anger, and Divine Smite still works, used without satisfaction. Disadvantage on Athletics requiring grip, Acrobatics requiring fine control, and Sleight of Hand from the damaged hands — never discussed.

Dav Ostric The man who detained Marek
▼
Visual description

Large, broad-shouldered, working clothes.

Goals for this encounter

Hand over custody and go home. Stepped in because it was the obvious thing to do — nobody asked him to.

Personality points

Doesn't resent Marek, just didn't want the festival disrupted. Thanks the party with the practical warmth of someone who's completed an obligation.

Others at the shrine Maren, Fen, Idris, Casimir, Brin
▼
Maren Ostric

Dav's wife. Arms folded, very patiently waiting to leave — didn't think any of this was worth the trouble, and might say so quietly if engaged.

Fen

Mid-teens helper, tidying up the aftermath. Wide-eyed, uncertain, useful for basic questions about the shrine and Morfudd.

Idris

Old drunk, seventies, amused rather than outraged — has outlasted several priests and festivals. More sympathetic to Marek than Morfudd, if asked.

Casimir

A pilgrim traveller who arrived for the ceremony. Has heard rumours of a wandering former paladin asking hard questions at shrines along the western roads — will share this if asked.

Brin

A child, eight or nine, profoundly disappointed the ceremony didn't happen, with strong opinions about the man who stopped it.

Encounter Beats
1 Arrival at the shrine ▼

The pool is the first thing the party sees — still, clear, offerings at the edge. Marek is visible near the cage at the south. Dav is nearby; Fen is tidying; Idris is sitting somewhere comfortable; Casimir is near the pool; Brin is somewhere between disappointed and angry; Maren wants to leave.

2 Free exploration ▼

This is the scene's purpose — getting comfortable with the environment and the VTT before anything consequential happens. A Religion check on the pool reveals Aeron's domain and the seeding ceremony's significance; a History check places the shrine in Brindle Hollow's community; a Perception check notices the ceremony preparations, including the animal that was never used. All NPCs are available and none are urgent.

3 Meeting Marek ▼

He receives the party with the calm patience of someone who's been waiting and isn't particularly bothered by how long it took. He answers questions without performing distress: plain and without heat on the ceremony, brief on his hands, accurate and brief on his faith. Not grateful, not hostile — simply present and ready to go.

4 Taking custody ▼

Dav hands over custody without drama, relieved to be done with it; Maren's expression makes her feelings clear regardless. Marek submits to whatever escort arrangement the party makes — bound, unbound, walking alongside — he has no preference. The party leaves the shrine. The road to Tallowhaven begins.

DM Notes
The missing fingersDon't mention them unless a player specifically looks at Marek's hands. If they do and ask, his answer is brief. The symbol his followers later make with their hands imitates this injury — the party won't know that yet. Plant it quietly and let it surface later.
Marek's oathHe's still technically a Paladin of Aeron and his oath is intact — his faith is the wound, not his vow. He won't identify himself as a paladin, but won't deny it if asked directly.
The shrine as seedAeron's absence is one of the campaign's hidden truths. Careful players may feel the space is slightly thin — worship without a clear recipient. Don't confirm this. Just let the feeling exist.
No rushThis scene has no clock. If the party spends an hour talking to Idris or playing cards with no one in particular, that's the scene working correctly.
