Combat Reference — The Rot

Four tiers of accumulated decay — they spread rather than kill — October 2026 rules
The Rotten condition

Stacks. A creature gains a stack each time it fails a save against the Rot. Each stack lowers one ability score by 2, always in this order: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma. Modifiers, saves, attacks, AC and HP follow the score.

Speed. From the first stack, speed is reduced by 10 ft. This does not increase with more stacks.

When to save. A Constitution save on contact with Rot and at the start of each of the creature's turns while still in contact.

Cap. Six stacks. Any further infestation at six drops the creature to 0 HP and normal dying rules apply. Recovering without magical healing leaves it at six.

Removal. Only magical healing (spell, prayer, potion or the ring) removes every stack at once. The Rot is forced out as a single dead Rot Dreg sized to the stacks removed. Rest removes nothing. There is no save to shake it off.

Immunity. Part-deified beings (Arjay, Dohyuk, Huw) never gain stacks, and healing expels nothing from them.
