Mission Reference — Act 1 / Trademasters / Second Mission
The Trumpet
Find the source of the article, deal with it quietly — before it grows legs
Investigation then combatLevel 3 partyEscalating pub fight — survivalHidden Courtesan connection

A fabricated article trashing the Trademasters' reputation runs in Tallowhaven's biggest paper, citing a research body that doesn't exist. Oskar tracks the party down himself -- a bad sign -- and wants the source found and dealt with before the festival meeting, not just a retraction. The trail leads through a mortified journalist to a wealthy young man who commissioned the smear, and from him to a pub fight that escalates in waves until the party realises just how much bigger the room actually was.

At a glance
Part 1 — Investigation
The article: cites a fabricated research body (no address, no charter, no civic record) claiming Trademaster work is overpriced and shoddy
Oskar's demand: not a retraction -- those get buried and unread. He wants the source found and removed before the festival meeting.
Calla (the journalist): defensive at first -- won't burn a source on request. Shown the fabricated research body, she turns cold and furious, and gives up the name herself.
The dead-end trap: stopping at Calla isn't enough. Oskar will send the party back out if they think suppressing the article solved anything.
Hidden layer: commissioned by the Courtesans Network through an intermediary who's now unreachable. Not discoverable this mission.
Part 2 — The Establishment
Perran Carew: the target -- entitled, drunk, and genuinely believes his own worldview. Swings first once he can't back down in front of his friends.
Escalation: three waves: Perran's table (round 1, mostly impaired), the bar (round 2, Brynn loyal, Mags dangerous and sober), the door and back (round 3, Davan Holt -- a sober, professional bodyguard with Action Surge)
The room empties: uninvolved patrons flee as the fight starts -- the party ends up fighting in an increasingly open space, the opposite of a cramped basement
Location Descriptions
The Trumpet's office

Where Calla works -- the byline on the article leads here directly.

The Establishment

A large, well-appointed festival-week tavern, bigger than it sounds -- round tables across the floor, a bar and kitchen along the left wall, a piano in the corner, and a prestige table under a mounted skull where Perran holds court. Busy: couples, trader groups, festival visitors, a working piano player.

NPCs
Oskar Sternov The employer — livid, finds the party, puts the paper on the table
▼
Personality points

Controlled fury, not theatrical -- precise. Has already done the checking himself; doesn't need the party to explain the article, needs them to find who's behind it.

Goals for this encounter

Get the source found and removed before the festival meeting -- not a retraction, which gets buried on page four.

What he keeps secret

If the party stops at Calla, explains patiently why that's not enough: the source still exists, the motivation still exists, The Trumpet isn't the problem.

Calla Journalist, The Trumpet — filed in good faith, was used
▼
Visual description

Has carved out a niche in a profession that didn't initially welcome her, and has become pragmatic as a result. Knows when she's being managed -- didn't catch it this time.

Goals for this encounter

Initially: protect her source, full stop, against anyone walking in with a grievance.

What she knows

Perran Carew's name and where he spends his festival evenings -- but not who fed him the idea or data beyond her own source.

What she keeps secret

Nothing once shown the evidence -- her fury afterward is genuine and pointed at Perran, not the party.

Personality points

Defensive and professional at first. Shown Oskar's documentation that the cited research body doesn't exist, she goes through genuine mortification into cold anger -- gives up the source not as a favour, but as her own first step toward understanding what was done to her. Already planning a response of her own.

Perran Carew Human commoner — the target, entitled, swings first
▼
Visual description

A young man of good Tallowhaven family who's confused inherited civic legacy -- a name etched in stone, a park, a scholarship -- with personal merit.

Goals for this encounter

Commissioned the article deliberately, dressed as research because he knows that's how you make something stick. Genuinely believes he thought of it himself.

What he knows

Was approached by a contact of a contact who mentioned the levy proposal and happened to have compelling data -- didn't question it because it confirmed what he already believed. Can't trace the chain any further.

Personality points

Verbatim worldview, given room to deliver it: “Who are they really? Nobodies, that's who. Their name hasn't been etched in stone for generations at the Aldermen's Hall. They don't have a park, a tavern, and a scholarship named after them, do they. They're nobodies. They'd do well to remember it.” When challenged, performs confidence rather than backs down -- lands badly in front of his friends, so he swings first. Frightened and quiet once beaten; agrees to retract.

AC 10, HP 18. Unarmed strike +2 (1d4+2). Impaired -- disadvantage on attack rolls and Dex saves.

Encounter Beats
I1 Oskar finds the party ▼

He doesn't do this lightly, and something about his manner tells the party it's serious before he sits down. He puts The Trumpet on the table, lets them read the headline, then points to the fabricated research body and states the facts plainly. Pays half upfront and tells them to find the source and deal with it -- not how.

I2 The Trumpet's office ▼

Calla receives the party with the wariness of a journalist who's had powerful people lean on her before, and stands by her story initially -- she won't give up a source. Presenting the evidence changes everything: this isn't suppression, it's a journalist discovering she was used. Her fury is genuine and pointed at Perran, and she gives the party his name and where he spends his festival evenings.

I3 The dead end -- if the party stops at Calla ▼

If they report back having only dealt with the article, Oskar receives it with measured patience and explains why it's not enough -- the source and the motivation both still exist. He doesn't send them away in frustration, just waits to see if they understand the distinction, and sends them back out if they don't.

C1 The confrontation ▼

The party finds Perran at his table and approaches with pointed questions. He receives them with the performance of someone above this kind of thing, and if given the space, delivers his worldview in full -- important for the party to hear before the fight. He can't back down in front of his friends, so he swings first.

C2 Round one -- the table ▼

Perran, Aldwyn, Osric, and Fenn -- all impaired except Perran, who's big enough that it barely matters. The room reacts immediately: a couple flees before Perran finishes his first swing, festival visitors scatter and knock over a table, the piano stops. The room empties fast, giving the party more space than they arrived with -- space that will feel very different once Davan Holt walks in.

C3 Round two -- the bar arrives ▼

Brynn and Mags enter. Brynn is straightforward; Mags is the real problem, targeting the most exposed party member and repositioning with Cunning Action. What felt manageable in round one no longer does.

C4 Round three -- door and back ▼
Source found, retraction secured, Oskar satisfied“Good work, cleanly done” -- high praise from him. Second half paid, the levy proposal moves forward, a buried correction runs that nobody reads.
Source found, retraction not securedOskar pays the second half since the source was found, pursues the retraction himself, and notes quietly that the job was half done.
Party stops at CallaSent back out, patiently, once -- the source is still out there.
Scene created, magistrate involvedOskar pays privately and later, doesn't acknowledge any public connection to the party, and isn't pleased.

Davan Holt enters from the door with a clear professional read on the room and goes straight for whoever's doing the most damage to Perran's side, using Action Surge if he needs to make a statement immediately. Jori enters from the back, swings once, and reconsiders his life choices. This is the survival round -- the party needs to end it fast, whether by dropping Perran, dropping Davan, or breaking the remaining commoners' will to continue.

DM Notes
The hidden Courtesan layerThe article was commissioned by the Courtesans Network through an intermediary who's now unreachable. Not discoverable this mission -- surfaces in the finale when the party looks back at all three second missions together.
Perran's speechGive him room to deliver it if the confrontation allows. It's the most important character beat in the encounter -- it reframes the fight from a pub brawl into something with real stakes, and the party should want to hit him before he finishes.
Davan HoltThe encounter's biggest tactical surprise. Eight combatants is already a lot; a sober fighter with Action Surge arriving in round three is the moment the party realises this was never going to be easy. Don't telegraph him.
MagsAt the bar by coincidence, not loyal to Perran -- disappears before resolution whether or not the party notices. Not their problem.
Fenn's exitLet it happen. A small, unwilling participant finding the earliest possible exit is a human moment worth playing briefly, at no cost to the tension.
Calla's next moveAlready planning her response to being used -- a future Trumpet story about a family with a habit of manufacturing convenient research. Doesn't affect this mission; the world just keeps going.
