Combat Reference — Northern Ambush

Broken Wing zealots — road just outside the north gate — Level 2 party of 5 — Deadly
Encounter behaviour

Davren watched the party argue with Marek and decided, entirely on his own, that they'd earned punishment for it. He's recruited five others who share his conviction to varying degrees. This is not a professional job — it's retribution, and Davren means it sincerely.

Davren leads from the front with the greatsword and does not retreat. Isha and Pytros use mobility and Pack Tactics to isolate whoever's alone. Cass, Bram, and Toll are true believers but not fighters — they came because they believe, not because they're any good at this.

Morale breaks unevenly. If the fight turns clearly against the group, Cass, Bram, and Toll will break and run once it's obvious they're losing. Davren and, to a lesser extent, Isha and Pytros, do not — their conviction runs deeper than the Converts'.

Scattered bouldersA few pieces of rock along the verge give Isha and Pytros something to duck behind between attacks.
