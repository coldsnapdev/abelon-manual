Combat Reference — The Driftwood Road

Radek's toll crew — bottleneck terrain — Level 3 party of 4 — Hard to Deadly
Encounter behaviour

This is a professional operation on ground Radek chose. The crew has sight lines, cover, and a position that funnels the party into a killzone. They are not here for a fight — they are here to extract payment — but they will commit fully if the party pushes it.

Radek holds the bottleneck. Morda and Brek work from elevated or flanking positions and do not come into melee willingly. Gort holds the line beside Radek. Thessaly controls the battlefield from cover. Peck stays at the rear with the ledger and is the objective, not a combatant.

Surrender is a real option. Radek will accept it. He would rather take coin than casualties. If the party is clearly losing he will offer terms before finishing them — he wants a reputation for professionalism, not murder.

Terrain — bottleneck road
The bottleneckRadek and Gort hold a narrow point on the road. The party cannot bring more than two fighters to bear at once without going off-road into difficult terrain.
Elevated positionsMorda and Brek occupy high ground on either side of the road. They have three-quarter cover and advantage on Perception checks. Moving to engage them costs the party additional movement through difficult terrain.
Thessaly's coverShe is behind the main group, in cover behind trees or a cart. She has half cover at minimum. She has line of sight to the road.
Peck's positionHe is furthest back, behind Thessaly. Reaching him requires getting through or around the entire crew first.
Radek
Fighter 3 — leader, holds the bottleneck
Tactics
Holds the bottleneck. Does not advance unless the party retreats. Uses Trip Attack to knock the most dangerous melee fighter prone and keep them in front of him. Uses Action Surge if two party members break through. Offers surrender terms before finishing a downed party member.
Thessaly
Mage — uncomfortable, controls the battlefield from cover
Thessaly has made choices that led here and is not entirely comfortable with them. If Radek goes down she disengages and does not continue fighting.
Tactics
Opens with Hold Person on the most effective melee combatant. Switches to Fog Cloud if ranged party members are decimating the flankers. Falls back to Magic Missile or Fire Bolt when concentration is unavailable. Uses Shield reaction to survive a hit if the party closes on her. Stays in cover. Does not move toward the fight.
Morda
Ranger 2 — elevated left flank, mobile
Tactics
Fires from elevation each round. Applies Hunter's Mark to whoever is most dangerous at range. Relocates along the ridge if a party member climbs toward her — she knows the terrain, they do not. Does not come down to melee unless forced.
Brek
Ranger 2 — elevated right flank, trapper
Tactics
More methodical than Morda. Focuses fire on whoever is already damaged. Uses Ensnaring Strike to lock down a party member trying to flank Radek. Older and less mobile — holds position rather than relocating. A party member who reaches him finds a more dangerous melee fighter than expected.
Gort
Fighter 1 — holds the line beside Radek
Tactics
Throws javelin on round one then holds the line. Does not move unless Radek moves. Has no strong opinions about anything except doing his job. Straightforward and effective.
Peck
Gnome — ledger keeper, objective not combatant
Peck has the ledger — the evidence the party needs. He is the objective. Reaching him ends the information-gathering phase of the encounter regardless of combat outcome.
Tactics
Stays as far from the fighting as possible. Runs if Radek goes down and he has a clear path. Does not run into the woods alone — he will move toward the road and try to put distance between himself and the party. If caught, he talks.
