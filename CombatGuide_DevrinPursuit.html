Combat Reference — The Devrin Pursuit

Devrin and Tobin, run to ground off the road — ad-hoc, not part of the original plan — Level 3–4 party — Deadly
Encounter behaviour

Devrin is retconned as a genuine spellcaster — Command and Silence, spell save DC 16 — not the purely martial Ops lead the checkpoint scene implied. This is the first time it's shown.

She opens with Silence the moment she's confronted, catching herself and any party spellcaster within 20 ft. Command is hers to use against whoever's blocking her retreat -- a single word to clear a path, not a spell she's saving for anyone in particular.

Against the party, she is not trying to win. Every choice is about buying distance: Hold Person on whoever's fastest, Inflict Wounds if cornered, retreat toward anything that breaks line of sight. She disengages the instant a route opens.

The Command-on-Yselle moment already happened. That's how the camp emptied out the night Arjay died -- resolved backstory, not part of this fight. Yselle isn't present here.

Tobin fights for real. A tough, bruising fighter — not the bystander the checkpoint scene suggested. He's protecting Devrin's escape, not trying to win outright, but he will hurt the party if they let him.

Terrain — off the road, wherever Devrin's chosen to make her stand
The groundA clearing, a ruined structure, or a stretch of wilderness away from the checkpoint and Halloway's people entirely — chosen deliberately for the retreat routes it offers.
The retreat lineDevrin picked this spot with an escape route in mind. If she reaches it, the fight shouldn't drag once she's on it.
Silence radius20 ft., centred where Devrin chooses on arrival. Mark it clearly on the map the moment it's cast — it will catch a party spellcaster who's closed distance too fast.
Tobin's positionPlants himself between the party and Devrin's escape route. Doesn't chase — holds ground and makes the party come through him.

Abilities
Silence (opens with this) — 20 ft. radius centred on a point she can see. No save. Suppresses verbal spell components for everyone inside it, herself included.
Command (DC 16 Wis save) — One word: "Flee," "Drop," or "Grovel." Used on whoever's physically blocking her retreat.
Hold Person (DC 16 Wis save) — Used on whoever's fastest, to buy an escape window.
Disengages on opening — The instant a clear route exists, she takes it rather than pressing any advantage. She is not trying to win this fight.
Tactics
Silence first, always — cast it the moment she's confronted, before anything else. Command on whoever's blocking her retreat line. Against the party: Hold Person on the fastest threat, Inflict Wounds only if physically cornered, retreat toward the escape line or anything breaking line of sight at every opportunity. Calm throughout — she does not raise her voice, does not gloat, does not explain unless directly asked and even then gives little away.
