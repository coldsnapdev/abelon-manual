Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Tallowhaven
The Aldermen's Hall
Delivering Marek, meeting Caen Rhys, and an accidental introduction to Alric Venn
Social encounterNo combatMerchant thread opens hereFirst arrival in Tallowhaven

The party delivers Marek to the magistrate, Caen Rhys, who takes testimony, fines him, and pays out Morfudd's fee. On the way through, Alric Venn -- the Merchants' Guildmaster, in the building on unrelated business -- notices the party and invites them to breakfast, opening the Merchant thread without the party realising quite how important the invitation is.

At a glance
Arrival: Pren at the entrance desk processes the party efficiently and directs them through the antechamber to the courtroom
Alric's introduction: incidental, not engineered -- he's here for his own business, notices the party, and suggests breakfast at the Shallow Tuber tomorrow. No pitch yet.
Caen Rhys's verdict: a nominal fine for public disorder; pays out the party's fee on Morfudd's behalf; asks them to commit to a future hearing that almost certainly won't be called
Marek's exit: taken below by Tev; his parting words are to Tev, not the party, and are precise, slightly unsettling, and unexplained
Location Descriptions
The Aldermen's Hall

A large, wide, low building with civic weight through its footprint rather than height -- solid and functional, making no attempt to impress. A raised wooden platform with six plain columns fronts the entrance; a noticeboard beside the door carries festival announcements and civic notices. Inside: worn flagstone floors, practical furniture, documents and ledgers on heavy desks. The party has access to the entrance, two antechambers, and the courtroom -- the grander main hall and private rooms beyond are not their business today.

The courtroom

Tiered seating, a raised magistrate's desk. Functional rather than grand.

NPCs
Caen Rhys Magistrate -- tired, fair, running out of resources
▼
Visual description

Late fifties, plain magistrate's robes, neat but not grand. Sharp eyes that have heard every excuse and weighed every claim.

Goals for this encounter

Process the Marek matter efficiently -- take testimony, issue a nominal fine, move on to the next thing on his list.

What he knows

Familiar with Marek-adjacent disruptions -- not the first shrine ceremony interrupted by someone making a point. Doesn't consider it worth more than a nominal fine.

Personality points

Brisk and fair, precise questions, expects precise answers. Not unfriendly, just busy -- the party is one of several things he's dealing with today. Tired but not cynical; takes his role seriously even when it doesn't reward him for it.

Alric Venn Merchants' Guildmaster -- present on his own business
▼
Visual description

Fifties, well dressed without ostentation -- the kind of wealth that doesn't need to announce itself. Calm, considered, always noting something.

Goals for this encounter

Not about Marek at all -- he's here because Caen summoned him over an outstanding festival-levy payment. Notices the party in passing and is curious about them.

What he knows

Has noticed capable-looking strangers delivering a prisoner. Provisionally interested in what he's seen, but doesn't oversell the meeting.

Personality points

Measured warmth -- interested but not eager. His small talk (where they're from, how they found the escort work) is assessment, not chat. Suggests breakfast at the Shallow Tuber tomorrow and leaves it there, no pressure.

Others at the Hall Pren, Oswin, Tev, Huw
▼
Pren (doorman)

Older, sharp eyes behind a tired face. Sorted the party into a category before they finished their first sentence -- efficient, not unfriendly, simply busy.

Oswin (civic guard)

Present in the courtroom during the hearing. Does nothing unless asked to.

Tev (gaoler)

Fifties, heavyset, keyring at his belt. Has locked up enough people to have stopped thinking about what put them there. Takes Marek without drama -- his parting words, if any, are to Tev, who receives them without visible reaction.

Huw (Alric's aide)

Mid-twenties, neat clothing slightly too well pressed, carrying documents. Doesn't speak unless spoken to. Watches everything Alric watches.

Encounter Beats
1 Arrival -- the noticeboard and Pren ▼

The noticeboard outside is worth a moment -- festival announcements, civic notices, a debt posting or two -- seeding Tallowhaven's commercial activity without needing explanation. Inside, Pren processes the party efficiently at the entrance desk; he knows what a citizen's-arrest delivery looks like and directs them through the antechamber to the courtroom to wait.

2 The antechamber -- Alric ▼

Alric is concluding or waiting on his own business and notices the party -- specifically their bearing, and that they're delivering a prisoner rather than in trouble themselves. He introduces himself briefly, asks a question or two that seem like small talk and aren't, then suggests breakfast at the Shallow Tuber tomorrow morning. He doesn't press, doesn't explain further, and moves on. Huw follows without speaking.

3 The courtroom -- Caen Rhys ▼

Caen takes their testimony: brisk, fair, precise questions about the arrest, the escort, and the ambush on the road if it happened. His verdict is a nominal fine for public disorder, paid before release -- standard. He asks the party to commit to attending a future hearing if one's called regarding any prisoners taken during the ambush; he doesn't expect to call it, but asks because that's the process. He pays out Morfudd's agreed fee on the party's behalf -- the magistrate holds payment to confirm delivery before funds change hands.

4 Marek's departure ▼

Tev collects Marek and takes him below. Marek's final words, if any, go to Tev rather than the party -- precise, slightly unsettling, and unanswered. The party doesn't see what happens next: Marek will be fined and released, and will walk out of the Hall later today on his own, without them.

5 Leaving the Hall ▼

The party is free, paid, and in Tallowhaven, with a breakfast invitation from a well-dressed man they met for four minutes -- that's the Merchant thread. The Amber Cup is the natural next stop; they need somewhere to stay, and Morra will see them settled.

DM Notes
Alric's questionsHis small talk is assessment -- where they're from, how they found the escort work, what they thought of the road. He's listening for confidence, self-awareness, and whether they're the kind of people who make problems or solve them, and has formed a provisional opinion before suggesting breakfast.
Caen Rhys in the finaleThe party sits across from this man again at the end of Act 1 under very different circumstances. Keep this first meeting unremarkable enough that the contrast lands when it comes -- he's fair now. Whether he's fair then is a question for later.
Marek's last wordsDecide what he says to Tev before running this scene. Something true, slightly abstract, and entirely unhelpful as a parting remark -- something he's been thinking about on the road. Tev doesn't engage with it; the party carries it if they heard it.
The hearing commitmentIt will almost certainly never be called. If it becomes relevant later -- if the magistrate needs leverage over the party during the Act 1 finale -- the commitment exists. File it away.
