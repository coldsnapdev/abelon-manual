Combat Guide — The Festival Finale

Cooper's Square — three faction paths — Level 3 party — Hard to Deadly
Encounter behaviour — all paths

The faction arrives in waves. The first wave is the most numerous but least capable — they create chaos and buy time for the real threats to commit. The faction leader hangs back until the crew takes significant losses, then commits with anger overcoming caution. If the leader is the last one standing they attempt to flee.

The fight is personal. These are not strangers. Whether the party pulls its blows, what they do when the leader tries to run, and how they treat the people who break — all of it matters and all of it has consequences in Act 2.

Alric's faction — Merchants

Huw leads. The polite assistant who booked their table is composed and cold in a way the party did not anticipate. Weapons skew toward quality purchased steel — longswords, rapiers, light crossbows. Huw has Charm Person. The merchants grabbed what was available.

Eight combatants total. Prys and Nesta break in round one. Bael and Gethin carry through wave one. Huw and Aldric are wave two. Alric arrives when Huw is in serious trouble.

Wave 1 — arrives immediately
Prys
Guild merchant — improvised weapon, breaks round 1
Breaks in round 1. More useful as environmental chaos than actual threat.
Swings once with whatever he picked up then looks for a reason to stop. Indignant fury covers the fact that he has never been in a fight.
Nesta
Guild merchant — breaks faster than Prys
Breaks immediately when first hit or when Prys breaks. Whichever comes first.
Follows Prys. Stops the moment she has a reason to. Does not need much of a reason.
Bael
Bodyguard — longsword and crossbow, professional
Moves toward the most dangerous party member immediately. Switches to crossbow if the party spreads out. Stays in longer than anyone expects.
Gethin
Bodyguard — shortsword, follows Bael entirely
Flanks whoever Bael is engaging. Breaks when Bael goes down — DC 10 Wis save or disengages.
Wave 2 — commits when wave 1 struggles
Huw
Alric's assistant — rapier, Charm Person — more capable than anyone knew
Huw has Charm Person. This is the moment the party realises he has not just been taking notes all these years.
Composed — Advantage on saves against being frightened.
Directs wave 1 from the back, draws rapier when Bael falls. Uses Charm Person immediately on his first turn in combat — targets whoever is dealing the most damage. Cold and deliberate throughout.
Aldric
Event coordinator — follows Huw, improvised weapon
Follows Huw's instructions. Does what he is told. Breaks if Huw goes down.
Wave 3 — arrives when Huw is in serious trouble
Alric Venn
Merchant Guild master — desperate energy, picked up weapon, never done this before
Alric has never fought before. He is frightened underneath the fury and will not show it. He commits with desperate energy — the most dangerous version of a man with nothing left to lose tonight.
Attempts to flee if last standing — DC 12 Athletics check to escape the square. Whether the party lets him matters in Act 2.
Targets whoever put Huw down. Does not fight cleverly — fights with feeling. Uses Suggestion only if cornered and out of other options. Attempts to flee if last standing.
Oskar's faction — Trademasters

Cors Holdworn leads. He was called before the meeting started — that level of preparation tells the party something about Oskar they did not fully appreciate. Weapons are craft tools and working weapons — warhammers, hand axes, mallets. Ysolde has Thunderwave. Nobody expects a master weaver to cast anything.

Seven combatants total. Mael breaks early. Tav and Efa Dun carry through wave one. Drest, Cors, and Ysolde are wave two — Ysolde casts once and leaves. Oskar arrives when Cors is struggling.

Wave 1 — arrives immediately
Roark
Journeyman carpenter — mallet, stubborn, breaks when Cors falls
Came for history. Stays for principle. Stubborn until Cors goes down then the reason to stay evaporates.
Mael
Journeyman potter — improvised, breaks early
Breaks when first hit or when any ally is incapacitated. Whichever comes first.
Follows Roark. Already reconsidering. Does not need much encouragement to stop.
Tav
Apprentice metalworker — hand axe, angry
Throws axe round one then closes to melee. Angry enough to stay longer than is wise. Holds until Cors goes down.
Efa Dun
Apprentice weaver — dagger, follows Cors
Follows Cors's lead entirely. Breaks when Cors goes down or steps sideways to the Sables (pink path only).
Wave 2 — commits alongside wave 1
Drest
Master blacksmith — warhammer, slower but dangerous
Grappler — +2 to Strength contests. Will try to grapple the most mobile party member to pin them.
Slower than the apprentices but hits considerably harder. Uses his size to control space. Holds until Oskar arrives or is incapacitated.
Cors Holdworn
Guild enforcer — heavy knife — the actual threat in wave 2
In the pink path, Cors steps sideways toward the Sables during wave 2. A quiet repositioning. Do not draw attention to it.
Threat Assessment — Identifies the most dangerous party member immediately. Targets them first and exclusively.
Professional and controlled. No wasted movement. Was called before the meeting started — he has been prepared all evening. The party will feel the difference between him and wave 1 immediately.
Ysolde
Master weaver — Thunderwave — casts once, walks away
Nobody expects a master weaver to cast anything. This is the most surprising moment of the teal path fight.
Casts Thunderwave once — aimed at the densest cluster of party members. Then turns and walks away without looking back. She is not a combatant. She spent sixty years building something and spent one spell defending it.
Wave 3 — arrives when Cors is struggling
Oskar Sternov
Trademaster — no weapon needed — cold anger, fights close and dirty
Oskar does not need a weapon. Built like someone who has worked with his hands for forty years. Cold anger rather than hot — harder to read than the others.
Powerful Build — Counts as one size larger for grappling purposes.
Attempts to flee if last standing — DC 12 Athletics to escape. Whether the party lets him matters in Act 2.
Goes for whoever put Cors down. Uses grapple to pin them against a wall or the ground while dealing with others. Cold and methodical. Does not shout. Does not waste energy. Attempts to flee if last standing.
Seva's faction — Courtesans

Elbeth arrives first. The party last saw her walking out of Coppergate Stables under her own power. She has had time to think about this moment. The Sables are trained and composed — the most dangerous wave one combatants across all three paths. Cors steps sideways from the Trademaster group during wave two. Seva is the most dangerous thing the party faces tonight.

Eight combatants total. The associates create chaos. The operatives and Mira apply real pressure. Cors's arrival is a surprise. Seva is the finale.

Wave 1 — arrives immediately
Elbeth Sable
Sable operative — shortsword, furious, has been waiting since Coppergate
Furious and unpredictable. The most emotionally charged combatant in the fight. May do things that are tactically suboptimal because she is angry.
Goes immediately for whoever she blames most for her time in Coppergate Stables. Does not break easily. Personal grievance overrides tactical sense.
Dalla Sable
Sable operative — shortsword, trained and composed
Flanks whoever Elbeth is engaging. Calm and efficient. Does not waste movement. The most tactically capable wave 1 combatant across all three paths.
Perys Sable
Sable operative — dagger, quick and light
Cunning Action (bonus) — Dash, Disengage, or Hide each turn.
Uses mobility rather than strength. Repositions every turn. Forces the party to split attention. Difficult to pin down.
Nance
Network operative — hand crossbow, experienced, already has a position
Has already scouted the square and chosen her position before the party arrived. Fires from cover each round. Entirely unhurried.
Wave 2 — commits when wave 1 struggles
Mira Sable
Sable — dagger, warm until she is not, then precise
Uncanny Poise — Does not provoke opportunity attacks when moving away.
Moves toward whoever is dealing the most damage. Nothing wasted. The party knew her as the warm face at the Amber Cup door. This version of her is different.
Cors Holdworn
Trademasters / Courtesans — steps sideways — surprise arrival
Cors steps away from the Trademaster group and toward the Sables during wave 2. A quiet repositioning. Do not draw attention to it unless a player notices.
Enters the fight on the Sable side. Targets whoever is engaging Mira. Professional and controlled.
Wave 3 — arrives when Mira is in trouble
Seva Sable
Courtesans Network — the most dangerous thing the party faces tonight
Seva is exactly as dangerous as the party always suspected she might be. Every movement has a purpose. She does not fight recklessly — she fights efficiently.
Uncanny Poise — Does not provoke opportunity attacks when moving away.
Composed — Advantage on saves vs frightened. Cannot be charmed by CR 2 or lower.
Threat Assessment — Identifies the most dangerous party member immediately on arrival.
Attempts to flee if last standing — DC 12 Athletics to escape. Whether the party lets her matters in Act 2.
Targets the most dangerous party member. Uses Read the Room on her first action to understand the battlefield before committing. Deflect reaction on any melee hit she sees coming.
Seva's Deflect reactionWhen Seva is hit by a melee attack she can see, she reduces the damage by (1d6+3). This is her existing stat block ability. Use it — it is the moment the party realises they are in a different kind of fight.
The escape attemptEach faction leader — Alric, Oskar, Seva — attempts to flee if last standing. Whether the party lets them go is a genuine moral choice with Act 2 consequences. Do not resolve it for them.
Cors Holdworn across pathsIn the teal path Cors fights for Oskar as wave 2. In the pink path he steps sideways to the Sables also in wave 2. In the blue path he is not present — he left with Anneth. His secret loyalty is a background thread. Only pull it if a player tugs at it.
Anneth HoldwornShe does not fight in any path. She may be present in Cooper's Square watching. She may have already left. Let the session decide. If she is present she watches everything with the quiet satisfaction of someone who has been patient for a very long time.
