Combat Reference — The Rot Monstrosity

Everbloom Catacombs, Floor 4 — the lake and the ring platform — Level 4 party — Deadly, long boss fight in three phases
Encounter behaviour

The Monstrosity is a literal chunk of Zareth given form, living in the lake of Rot at the bottom of the catacombs. It has no mind, so no fear, command or other cognitive effect ever works on it. The fight should feel incredibly challenging: achievable but not easy, and long enough to cap the crawl. It is built to avoid a two-round collapse, so the phases below control the pace.

It fights on the ring platform in the lake, which has one narrow stair on and off. The keepers' torches along the semicircle before the platform are why it stays in the lake, and they do not stop it in combat.

Its Rot DC is 17, plus 1 for every stack a creature already has. The cap is six stacks, and any further infestation drops the character to 0 HP. Magical healing clears every stack, and Healing Word expels the stacks as a dead Dreg. The Officiant's Stole gives advantage on Rot saves, so a party that rested and attuned at the altar is much better placed. The party should be level 4 by this fight.

Terrain — the arena below the altar chamber
The lakeThick, glossy black Rot on every side of the circular platform, moving in slow swirls with steam rising. Anyone knocked or shoved in makes a Constitution save on entry and at the start of each turn in it: DC 12 below the waist, DC 14 waist to head, DC 17 fully under, each +1 per stack. Climbing out is Athletics DC 12.
The circular platformThe party's ground for the fight, with one narrow stair on and off. Everything else is lake.
The torchesTorches line the semicircular platform before the circle. A torch pulled from its bracket is an improvised weapon that deals fire damage. The Monstrosity is vulnerable to fire, so fire damage is doubled.
The darkBeyond the circle, the far walls are swallowed by darkness. The Monstrosity's blindsight reaches 120 ft.
Fight structure — three phases
Phase 1The Monstrosity stands on the platform with no legendary actions. Surge twice a turn, Corroding Presence, Harden when it recharges. Ends when it reaches half HP (68), at which point it sinks into the lake.
Phase 2: the swarmAt the start of each round for 3 rounds, 1d4+1 Rot Skulkers climb out of the lake. The phase ends at the earlier of the party's 10th Skulker kill or the end of round 3, and surviving Skulkers slip back into the lake. This is also the party's breather: healing (Healing Word expels Rot stacks), repositioning and grabbing torches.
Phase 3The Monstrosity resurfaces on its remaining HP with all 3 legendary actions active (Pulse, Expel, Surge). Harden, Effluent and Thunder Fission all stay live.
Thunder reactionJenok has a thunder reaction. Expect him to use it once, trigger Thunder Fission and teach the table what it does. Fragments enter initiative as the tier matching their HP.
Rot Monstrosity
Large ooze, unaligned — the dungeon's final encounter
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing, psychic. Immune: necrotic, which heals it instead. Immune to blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, prone, restrained, unconscious.
Tactics
Phase 1: it holds the platform and presses whoever is nearest, with Survival Instinct pulling it onto anyone who has dealt it fire or radiant damage. Surge twice on the same target where possible, since two failed saves can drop a character several stages in one round. Use Harden as soon as it recharges while a melee fighter is engaged, so every hit costs the attacker 2d8 necrotic. At half HP it sinks and Phase 2 begins. Phase 3: it resurfaces on its remaining HP with legendary actions available. Spend them after the party's turns: Pulse to close on someone who stepped back, Expel when a caster is standing off, Surge when anyone is within 10 ft. Expelled Dregs spawn next to the weakest-looking target.
Running the damage types
Necrotic heals it, so anyone using necrotic damage feeds it. Acid and lightning splash back onto anyone close, so ranged users are safer. Thunder splits it, so a loud party fills the platform with fragments. Fire and radiant are the clean answers. Cold slows it and may freeze it, which buys an hour and gives the party time to finish it with fire or salt.
Rot Skulker
Small ooze — phase 2 swarm, 1d4+1 climb out each round for 3 rounds
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing. Immune: necrotic (heals it), psychic.
Tactics
They climb out at the lake's edge at the start of each round and lunge at the nearest creature. No retreat until the phase ends. The party needs 10 kills to end the phase early, which on average takes most of the three rounds. Survivors slip back into the lake.
Rot Dreg
Tiny ooze — expelled by the Monstrosity, or split from it by thunder
Vulnerable: radiant, fire. Resistant: bludgeoning (also Slowed), slashing, piercing. Immune: necrotic (heals it), psychic.
Tactics
Each Dreg is a small hazard on the platform, and an expelled one enters initiative immediately. It drifts toward whoever is closest. A character who kills one takes nothing for it. The danger is standing in contact at the end of a turn with the Monstrosity's aura already running.
