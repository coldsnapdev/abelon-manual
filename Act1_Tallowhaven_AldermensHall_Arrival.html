Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Tallowhaven
The Aldermen's Hall
Delivering Marek, meeting Caen Rhys, and an accidental introduction to Alric Venn
Social encounter No combat Merchant thread opens here First arrival in Tallowhaven
Environment

The Aldermen's Hall is a large, wide, low building on a cobbled street in Tallowhaven's civic quarter. It has civic weight through its footprint rather than its height — solid and functional, the size of a prosperous merchant's townhouse multiplied, making no attempt to impress. A raised wooden platform with six plain columns fronts the entrance. A noticeboard beside the door carries festival announcements and civic notices.

Inside: worn flagstone floors, practical furniture, documents and ledgers on heavy wooden desks. The building houses several civic functions — the magistrate's court, a records office, holding cells below. It has the atmosphere of a place where practical decisions are made by tired people with limited resources.

The party has access to the entrance, two antechambers, and the courtroom. The grander main hall and private rooms beyond are not their business today.

Entrance Pren is at the desk. He has seen everyone and forgotten nobody. He controls access to the building with the calm efficiency of someone who has been doing this longer than most magistrates.
Antechambers Waiting areas with benches. Where Alric Venn will be found — present on his own business, not Marek's. The party passes through here before the courtroom.
Courtroom Tiered seating, a raised magistrate's desk. Functional rather than grand. Caen Rhys belongs behind that desk. The party and Marek stand before him.
Holding cells Below, accessed through a door the party does not go through. Tev takes Marek down here. The party does not see where Marek goes next.
Noticeboard Festival notices, civic announcements, debt postings. A natural thing for curious players to investigate on the way in. Seeds the festival without requiring exposition.
The wider building The grand main hall and staircase exist but the party does not access them today. Their presence implies the building has more to offer in future sessions.

Encounter Beats
Purpose of this sceneThe party delivers Marek, completes the first job, gets paid eventually, and accidentally meets the most important guild contact in Tallowhaven. The Alric introduction should feel incidental rather than engineered. He notices them. They are worth noticing.
01 Arrival — the noticeboard and Pren ▼

The party arrives at the Aldermen's Hall. The noticeboard outside is worth a moment — festival announcements, civic notices, a debt posting or two. It seeds Tallowhaven's commercial activity without requiring explanation.

Inside, Pren at the entrance desk processes them efficiently. He knows what a citizen's arrest delivery looks like. He directs them through the antechamber to the courtroom and tells them to wait.

02 The antechamber — Alric ▼

Alric Venn is in the antechamber, concluding or waiting on his own business. He notices the party — specifically their bearing and the fact that they are delivering a prisoner rather than in trouble themselves. He introduces himself briefly, shakes hands, asks a question or two that seem like small talk and are not.

He suggests breakfast at the Shallow Tuber tomorrow morning. He will explain what he has in mind then. He does not press. He moves on.

Huw follows without speaking.

03 The courtroom — Caen Rhys ▼

Caen Rhys takes their testimony from behind his desk. He is brisk and fair. He asks precise questions about the arrest, the escort, and the ambush on the road if it occurred. He writes things down.

His verdict on Marek: a nominal fine for public disorder, to be paid before release. Standard. He has seen this kind of thing before and has an efficient process for it.

He asks the party to commit to attending a future hearing if one is called regarding any prisoners taken during the ambush. He does not expect to call it. He asks because that is the process.

He pays Morfudd's agreed fee to the party on behalf of the arrest. This is also standard — the magistrate holds the payment to ensure delivery is completed before funds change hands.

04 Marek's departure ▼

Tev collects Marek and takes him below. Marek's final words before he goes are to Tev rather than to the party. They may be overheard. They are precise and slightly unsettling and Tev does not respond to them. He simply walks Marek through the door.

The party does not see what happens next. Marek is processed. He will be fined and released. He will walk out of the Aldermen's Hall later today and continue on his way. The party will not be there for that.

05 Leaving the Hall ▼

The party is free to go. They have been paid. They are in Tallowhaven. The day is theirs.

They have a breakfast invitation from a well-dressed man they met for four minutes. That is the Merchant thread.

The Amber Cup is the natural next destination — they need somewhere to stay and Morra will see them settled. The evening begins there.

DM Notes
Alric's questionsHis small talk is assessment. He asks where they are from, how they found the escort work, what they thought of the road. He is listening for confidence, self-awareness, and whether they are the kind of people who make problems or solve them. He has formed a provisional opinion before he suggests breakfast.
Caen Rhys in the finaleThe party will sit across from this man again at the end of Act 1 under very different circumstances. Their first meeting should be unremarkable enough that the contrast lands properly when it comes. He is fair now. Whether he is fair then is a question for later.
Marek's last wordsDecide what Marek says to Tev before you run this scene. It should be something true, slightly abstract, and entirely unhelpful as a parting remark. Something he has been thinking about on the road. Tev does not engage with it. The party carries it if they heard it.
The hearing commitmentThe party commits to attending a future hearing. It will almost certainly never be called. If it becomes relevant later — if the magistrate needs leverage over them during the Act 1 finale — the commitment exists. File it away.
