Encounter Reference — Act 1 / Road from Brindle Hollow
Ambush on the Road
Broken Wing sympathisers try to free Marek — Seva Sable watches
Combat encounterFirst combat, level 1 partyMoral weight: low (raises later)

Four of Marek's sympathisers ambush the party's escort at a bridge, trying to free him without killing anyone. The party fends them off (or doesn't), and a courtesan named Seva Sable — who talked her way into travelling with the ambushers the night before — watches the whole thing from a professional distance, filing the party away as someone worth knowing.

At a glance
Setup: a stone bridge chokepoint over a shallow stream; Pell holds it with a crossbow, Brath and Davan charge from the near side, Idony flanks from the riverbank
Shared goal: neither side wants this lethal — the ambushers want Marek free, not dead; the party wants to deliver him, not necessarily fight
Retreat trigger: once two ambushers are down, Brath calls retreat — they disengage rather than fight to the death
Seva Sable: not a combatant. Composed, watching, filing the party away as a professional assessment — she'll turn up again at the Amber Cup
The ruin: an unexplained circular structure nearby — a drained Zarethian wellspring nobody in-world has context for. Leave it as an unexplained feeling.
Location Descriptions
The bridge

Stone, narrow, a natural chokepoint. High ground and clear sightlines down the road.

The stream

Shallow, but difficult terrain if crossed.

The ruin

A large circular stone structure to the northwest — dry, and slightly wrong in a way nobody can place.

Open ground

Room to manoeuvre west of the bridge. Sheep scattered across it — running through them causes minor chaos.

NPCs
Brath Fighter 1 — de facto leader, charges from the bridge
▼
Visual description

Late twenties, lean, leather armour worn practically — a face that made a decision and stopped questioning it.

Goals for this encounter

Reach Marek and create space for him to escape. Calls retreat once two ambushers are down — does not fight to the death.

Personality points

Uses Tactical Shout to direct allies; last to disengage; Second Wind if he drops below 6 HP.

Combat

AC 13, HP 12. Longsword +5, 1d8+3 slashing. Second Wind (1/short rest): 1d10+2 HP as bonus action. Tactical Shout (recharge 5–6): one ally moves their speed without opportunity attacks.

Idony Rogue 1 — flanks from the riverbank, true believer
▼
Visual description

Mid-twenties, light leather, calm calculation rather than anger — the most ideologically committed of the four.

Goals for this encounter

If Marek is being physically held, targets that character specifically to break the hold.

Personality points

Believes in what she's doing even if she can't fully articulate Marek's argument. Most likely of the four to escape — disengages if Brath goes down.

Combat

AC 13, HP 8. Shortsword +4, 1d6+2 piercing + 1d6 Sneak Attack. Dagger thrown 1d4+2. Cunning Action (Dash/Disengage/Hide as bonus action).

Pell Crossbow — holds the bridge, least committed
▼
Visual description

Early twenties, no armour, plain clothes.

Goals for this encounter

Holds the bridge because Brath holds the bridge — not because he particularly believes in this.

Personality points

Reluctant: if Brath goes down and Pell is below half HP, DC 10 Wisdom save or he flees.

Combat

AC 11, HP 9. Club 1d4+1 / Light crossbow 1d8 (range 80/320ft).

Davan Axes — charges with Brath, goes down swinging
▼
Visual description

Thirties, leather armour — came from somewhere else and found Marek's ideas when he needed them most.

Goals for this encounter

The most physically committed of the four. Throws a handaxe round one, then goes melee.

Personality points

Scrapper: first time he'd drop to 0 HP, DC 10 Con save to drop to 1 HP instead (once per long rest).

Combat

AC 12, HP 11. Handaxe melee or thrown, 1d6+2 slashing (range 20/60ft).

Seva Sable Courtesans Network — witness, not combatant
▼
Visual description

Warm, unhurried, not defensive — nothing about her composure fits a roadside ambush.

Goals for this encounter

Talked her way into the group the night before after one of them spoke too openly at an inn. Deciding what the party might be useful for.

What she knows

Has no stake in whether Marek goes free — she's here for leverage and intelligence, not ideology.

What she keeps secret

Doesn't explain her presence unless pressed, and is economical with the truth even then.

Personality points

Doesn't fight, flee, or hide — watches with the attentiveness of a professional assessment. If threatened, calmly makes clear that harming her would be a mistake. She's correct.

Combat (if forced)

AC 13, HP 6. Dagger +4, 1d4+2 piercing. Composed: advantage on saves vs frightened. Read the Room (action): DC 12 Insight on a creature to learn emotional state, duress, or concealed weapons.

Encounter Beats
1 The party crests the rise ▼

The party comes over a rise and sees the bridge ahead: Pell on it, Brath and Davan visible on the near side, Idony at the riverbank, Seva slightly apart, watching. A successful Perception check reveals the group's positioning is deliberate, not incidental.

Marek, behind the party, observes with mild interest and does not warn them.

2 Brath and Davan charge ▼

Brath and Davan move down from the bridge. Davan throws a handaxe on the first round. Pell fires from the bridge. Idony repositions along the riverbank for an angle. Roll initiative.

3 The fight ▼

The ambushers focus on Marek's escape, not the party's destruction — Brath creates space with Tactical Shout, Idony targets whoever's closest to Marek, Pell suppresses from the bridge. Marek stands wherever he was when the fight started and watches with the expression of someone waiting for a cart to pass. He does not help either side.

Sheep cause minor chaos if anyone runs through them — disadvantage on the next attack roll from the distraction is a reasonable call.

Once two ambushers are down, Brath calls retreat and they disengage. Idony is most likely to escape; Pell is most likely to simply run.

4 Aftermath ▼
Standard — ambushers repelled, Marek still in custodyThe escort continues to Tallowhaven. Seva finds them at the Amber Cup.
Marek released or escapesHe walks away without ceremony, but gives the party a contact in the Trademasters' Guild before he goes — not gratitude, just information he thinks they'll find useful. The party arrives in Tallowhaven without a prisoner and without Merchant access through the Aldermen's Hall.
Marek killed (unlikely but possible)Seva witnessed it and won't approach on the road — she finds them in Tallowhaven instead, pleasant and threat-free, curious about the kind of people who make that choice. The Courtesan thread opens from an unusual angle.
Prisoners takenThe magistrate in Tallowhaven takes brief testimony: a fine for affray, brief detention, no further consequence. See the Aldermen's Hall encounter for the magistrate scene.

The party may have prisoners, questions, and will almost certainly notice Seva if they haven't already. She doesn't attempt to leave — she was never hiding. She waits to see what they do next.

If asked what the ambush was about, Marek gives an accurate and unhelpful answer: he didn't organise it, didn't ask for it, and assumes they were persuaded by his arguments. He's wrong about that, but doesn't know it.

DM Notes
The ruinDon't explain it. Players who investigate find a dry circular structure that feels slightly wrong, with no obvious reason, history, or danger. It's a drained Zarethian wellspring — nobody in the world has context for that. Leave it as a feeling.
Seva's composureHer calm during the fight is the tell — the most composed person on the road, and not fighting. Players who notice will file it away; players who don't will meet her properly at the Amber Cup either way.
The Broken Wing connectionThe ambushers show the linked-thumbs gesture at some point — a handshake between two of them, a gesture across the field. It means nothing to the party yet. It will mean something later.
Marek's indifferenceHe watches with mild interest and does nothing, even if the party is clearly losing. If asked why, his answer is precise and slightly maddening: he didn't ask to be rescued and sees no reason to assist his own re-arrest. Both things are true for him at once.
